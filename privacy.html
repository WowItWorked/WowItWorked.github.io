PRIVACY

Privacy

This site has no accounts, no sign-up, no forms, and no advertising. It does not set tracking cookies and does not sell or share visitor data.

What is collected

Hosting logs. The site is served by GitHub Pages through Cloudflare. Like any web server, those services receive your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request, and may keep standard access logs for security and operation under their own policies: GitHub, Cloudflare.

Aggregate analytics. The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies, fingerprinting, or tracking across sites. The author sees aggregate numbers only.

Fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font files are requested. See Google's font privacy notice.

What stays on your device

Nothing. Readiness Signal and this home page use no cookies, local storage, or other browser storage.

What is not done

No advertising networks, no social-media pixels, no third-party trackers, no email list, no newsletter. Outbound links to sources go to those sites' own pages under their own policies.
