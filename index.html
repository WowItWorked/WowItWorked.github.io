Readiness Signal

A filter, not a feed. Four scheduled runs a day read public sources — regulators, standards bodies, industry and threat research, and news — and publish only what clears a deliberately high materiality bar. Each item carries exactly one mechanism saying what it asks of the reader. When nothing clears, the run is silent. The archive is append-only, and the selection standard, runbook, and source registry are published with the code.

Cyber & Tech Risk Quantification Platform

An interactive prototype of a cyber & technology risk quantification platform: FAIR-based Monte Carlo simulation with a real in-browser engine, decision-first governance, and regulator-grade defensibility — reproducible seeded runs, provenance-tiered inputs, and an append-only challenge ledger. A role switcher shows the platform as the first line, second line, model risk, and executives would each see it. All data is simulated.
