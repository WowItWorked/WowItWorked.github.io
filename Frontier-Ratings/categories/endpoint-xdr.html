Why it matters to a bank

  • Ransomware and hands-on-keyboard intrusions start on endpoints
  • A faulty security update can take down the bank's own machines
  • Detection signals are spread across tools and missed

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-02-17

    Palo Alto Networks signed a definitive agreement to acquire Koi, a startup securing AI agents and tools that run on endpoints, and said Koi's technology would be added to its Prisma AIRS platform and its Cortex XDR endpoint product. The price was not disclosed. Palo Alto Networks (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  2. 2025-10-14

    Managed security services provider LevelBlue announced a proposed acquisition of XDR vendor Cybereason, with SoftBank becoming an investor in LevelBlue as part of the transaction; the price was not disclosed. GovInfoSecurity (ISMG) (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  3. 2025-06-27

    Microsoft said it would give some Microsoft Virus Initiative partners a private preview, from July 2025, of a Windows endpoint security platform that lets anti-malware and endpoint detection products run in user mode rather than in the kernel. The work is part of the Windows Resiliency Initiative that followed the July 2024 CrowdStrike update incident; vendors Microsoft said it worked with include CrowdStrike, Bitdefender, ESET, SentinelOne, Sophos, Trellix, Trend Micro and WithSecure. SecurityWeek (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  4. 2025-02-04

    Sophos completed its $859 million all-cash acquisition of Secureworks, whose shares stopped trading on Nasdaq, and said it would combine Secureworks' Taegis XDR platform with its own managed detection and response services. SecurityWeek (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  5. 2024-12-16

    Arctic Wolf agreed to buy the Cylance endpoint security business from BlackBerry for $160 million; BlackBerry had paid $1.4 billion for Cylance in 2018. BankInfoSecurity (ISMG) (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)
