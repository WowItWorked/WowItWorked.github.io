Why it matters to a bank

  • Ransomware or a destructive attack encrypts or deletes production data and the backups with it
  • The bank restores from a copy that still contains the attacker's malware or changes, and is reinfected
  • Recovery plans are untested, so critical services cannot be restored within the bank's impact tolerances

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-11-22

    Backup vendors now sell recovery from cyberattack, not only from failure. The product pages read describe the same pattern: copies that cannot be altered, scanning of backups for anomalies and known threats, recovery into an isolated or clean environment, and rehearsed recovery plans with reports. Commvault presents separate offerings for clean isolated recovery, cloud, SaaS and identity systems; Veeam describes immutable and vaulted copies, malware and indicator scanning, and orchestrated recovery with automated testing; Rubrik documents threat analytics, orchestrated application recovery and identity recovery in one cloud service. Two directions stand out: recovery of identity systems (Active Directory, Entra ID, Okta) is treated as the first step of any recovery, and vendors are adding recovery from damage done by AI agents. Rubrik's annual report names Commvault, Dell EMC, IBM, Veeam and Cohesity as competitors.
