Why it matters to a bank

  • Authorised push payment scams and account takeover cause direct customer loss
  • Transaction monitoring generates more alerts than investigators can review
  • Model decisions must be explainable to customers and supervisors

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-11-04

    NICE said on its third-quarter earnings call that the sale process for its Actimize financial-crime business was continuing and gave no update, as reported by Calcalist. Calcalist noted a September report that Brookfield had entered exclusive talks to buy Actimize for about $2 billion, which NICE has not confirmed. Calcalist CTech (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-08-03

    Visa agreed to acquire behavioural-biometrics fraud vendor BioCatch for $2.4 billion in cash from funds advised by Permira and other shareholders, with closing expected by the end of Visa's fiscal second quarter of 2027, subject to regulatory approval. SecurityWeek (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  3. 2025-11-27

    European Parliament and Council negotiators reached a provisional deal on the EU Payment Services Regulation and PSD3. Under it, a payment service provider that fails to put appropriate fraud-prevention mechanisms in place is liable for customers' losses, providers must check that a payee's name matches the account identifier, and victims of bank-impersonation fraud must be refunded in full. The deal still needed formal adoption by both institutions. European Parliament (regulator or standards body, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  4. 2024-10-07

    The UK Payment Systems Regulator's reimbursement protections for victims of authorised push payment scams took effect for Faster Payments and CHAPS payments made on or after 7 October 2024. The standard protection covers up to GBP 85,000, and the regulator said most consumers can expect reimbursement within five business days of a claim. Payment Systems Regulator (regulator or standards body, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  5. 2024-09-27

    Visa agreed to acquire Featurespace, a developer of real-time AI fraud and financial-crime detection for payments that grew out of Cambridge University's engineering department, with closing expected in Visa's fiscal year 2025; terms were not disclosed. SecurityWeek (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)
