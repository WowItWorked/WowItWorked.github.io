Why it matters to a bank

  • Agents act with more authority than intended
  • There is no reliable record of what an agent did and why
  • Agents chain together tools and data in ways nobody reviewed

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-02-17

    NIST's Center for AI Standards and Innovation launched the AI Agent Standards Initiative on 17 February 2026, with three strands: supporting industry-led agent standards, encouraging community-led open-source agent protocols, and research on agent security and identity. Alongside it NIST sought input through a request for information on AI agent security (due 9 March) and a concept paper on agent identity and authorisation (due 2 April). National Institute of Standards and Technology (regulator or standards body, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-02-06

    UiPath announced on 6 February 2026 that it had acquired WorkFusion, whose pre-built AI agents handle first-level analyst work in financial crime compliance, including sanctions-screening alert review, adverse media monitoring, KYC and transaction-monitoring investigations. Terms were not disclosed. UiPath (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  3. 2025-12-15

    ServiceNow announced on 15 December 2025 that it had completed its acquisition of Moveworks, maker of an employee AI assistant, enterprise search and a reasoning engine for agentic AI. The completion release did not state a price. ServiceNow (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  4. 2025-11-06

    Automation Anywhere announced it would acquire Aisera, which builds self-service AI agents for IT service management, HR and customer service, as reported by KMWorld on 6 November 2025; the report gave no price or closing date. KMWorld (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  5. 2025-05-15

    Salesforce agreed to acquire Convergence.ai, a start-up founded in 2024 that builds task-automation agents, to advance its Agentforce platform, as reported by CIO Dive on 15 May 2025. Terms were not disclosed and Salesforce expected the deal to close in its fiscal quarter ending 31 July 2025. CIO Dive (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

Recent events

  1. 2026-11-19 · launch LangChain
    LangChain puts LangSmith Tool Gateway into public beta

    LangChain announced on 2026-11-19 a public beta of LangSmith Tool Gateway, a managed layer between agents and their tools that handles credentials, identity and access for each tool call and traces the calls in LangSmith. According to the post, deny policies and the audit log require contacting LangChain, and the audit log is an Enterprise plan feature. LangChain (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-11-19 · partnership Salesforce
    Salesforce says US Commerce Department selected its consortium for a technology export programme

    Salesforce announced on 2026-11-19 that the US Department of Commerce had selected a consortium it leads as one of 33 initial consortia in the American SI Exports Program, which Salesforce says brings priority government promotion to foreign buyers and priority export-control review. Agentforce is one component of the package, alongside Hyperforce, Data 360, Informatica and the Einstein Trust Layer. Other consortium members are not named, and no Commerce Department notice was read. Salesforce (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  3. 2026-11-17 · launch LangChain
    LangSmith adds access controls for trace inputs and outputs on Enterprise plans

    LangChain announced on 2026-11-17 three access controls for LangSmith: restricting who can see trace inputs and outputs, user groups with per-workspace permissions, and policies that decide trace visibility from run metadata such as a customer ID. The company says the controls are available on LangSmith Enterprise plans and that traces are still stored in full. LangChain (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  4. 2026-11-16 · launch LangChain
    LangChain reorganises LangSmith around a per-agent record and announces the LangSmith Agent Platform

    LangChain announced on 2026-11-16 that LangSmith has been rebuilt around an agent record: each agent gets an owner, an immutable ID and a type, with environments and traces grouped under it. The company says agents on any OpenTelemetry-compatible platform can be registered alongside those built in LangSmith, and that the new version is available in cloud, bring-your-own-cloud and self-hosted deployments. No pricing change is mentioned. LangChain (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  5. 2026-11-13 · funding LangChain
    LangChain announces US$300 million Series C at US$3 billion valuation

    LangChain announced on 2026-11-13 a US$300 million Series C at a US$3 billion valuation led by existing investor CapitalG, with new investors including GIC and DFJ Growth. It said more than 7,000 organisations use LangSmith. LangChain (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  6. 2026-11-10 · certification Pegasystems
    Pega Cloud for Government receives GovRAMP Impact Level High status

    Pegasystems announced on 2026-11-10 that Pega Cloud for Government had received GovRAMP Impact Level High status after an independent assessment, making the service available under that programme to US state and local governments. The release does not say which Pega Platform features, including AI or agent features, are in scope. The GovRAMP listing itself was not read. Pegasystems (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  7. 2026-11-10 · partnership UiPath
    UiPath named a launch partner for Google Cloud's Gemini for healthcare

    In a press release dated 2026-11-10, UiPath said it is a launch partner for Google Cloud's Gemini for healthcare. The UiPath MCP Server lets Gemini users start and monitor automations and hand work to agents that run on the UiPath Platform under the customer's existing permissions and audit controls. UiPath said the server is in preview for joint UiPath and Google Cloud customers. UiPath (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  8. 2026-10-20 · acquisition Salesforce
    Salesforce signs definitive agreement to acquire Braze for about US$5.66B; deal not yet closed

    Salesforce announced in a press release dated 2026-10-20 that it had signed a definitive agreement to acquire Braze, a customer engagement software company, for US$47.50 in cash per Class A share, which it put at about US$5.66 billion in enterprise value. Salesforce said it expects closing in its fiscal year 2028, subject to approval by Braze shareholders, regulatory approvals and other customary conditions, and that it plans to build Braze's engagement and decisioning technology into Data 360, Customer 360 (including Marketing Cloud) and Agentforce. The deal had not been completed when this was recorded. Salesforce (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)
