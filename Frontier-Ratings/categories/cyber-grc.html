Why it matters to a bank

  • Control evidence is gathered by hand before each audit or examination, so it is out of date between reviews
  • The same control is documented and tested separately for every regulation and framework, in spreadsheets that disagree
  • Findings from security tools are not tied to the controls, risks and remediation actions that examiners ask about

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-11-23

    Vendors in this market describe the same core: a common control set mapped to many frameworks, risk assessments, control testing with evidence, policies and issue tracking on one platform. MetricStream, Archer and Hyperproof all present their products this way; MetricStream describes AI agents that test controls and gather evidence, and Hyperproof describes its modules as AI-powered. The difference being drawn is how controls are tested: RegScale positions continuous controls monitoring, fed by integrations with security tools and machine-readable formats such as NIST OSCAL, against periodic assessment, and Archer describes pulling control test results from automated systems alongside manual evidence. Money and sector bodies are following: SecurityWeek reported a Series B of more than US$30 million for RegScale in September 2025, and in February 2025 the Cyber Risk Institute said financial institutions want to move from point-in-time assessments to continuous control monitoring.
