Why it matters to a bank

  • VPNs give broad network access once a user is in
  • Traffic to cloud and AI services bypasses inspection
  • The security service itself becomes a single point of failure

Where the category is heading

  1. 2025-09-17

    Netskope went public: its final prospectus dated 17 September 2025 offered 47.8 million Class A shares at $19.00 each, listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol NTSK. US Securities and Exchange Commission (primary filing, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  2. 2025-09-03

    Cato Networks made its first acquisition, buying Aim Security, a company founded in 2022 to help organisations deploy generative-AI tools securely, for an undisclosed price, and extended its Series G to $409 million with a further $50 million from Acrew Capital. Cato said it planned to offer Aim's capabilities inside its SASE platform in early 2026. SecurityWeek (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  3. 2025-06-30

    Cato Networks raised $359 million in a Series G round at a $4.8 billion valuation, led by new investors Vitruvian Partners and ION Crossover Partners, as reported by Globes; its valuation at the previous round was $3 billion. Globes (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

    CISA, with the FBI and the national cyber agencies of New Zealand and Canada, published guidance on modern approaches to network access security. It sets out the risks of traditional remote access and VPN deployments and encourages organisations of all sizes to move towards zero trust, security service edge (SSE) and secure access service edge (SASE). Recent events

  1. 2026-11-17 · launch Cato Networks
    Cato Networks announces AgentSense runtime guardrail for AI agents

    Cato Networks announced on 2026-11-17, in a release distributed by PR Newswire, a capability called AgentSense that assesses AI agents while they run and aims to block unsafe actions, including those steered by untrusted content. Cato said it is integrated in the Cato platform and included at no extra charge in Cato AI Usage licences; a drift detection function was described as still to come. Cato Networks (via PR Newswire) (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-11-10 · vulnerability disclosure Fortinet
    Fortinet discloses two critical routing-daemon flaws affecting FortiOS and FortiSASE

    According to Fortinet PSIRT advisories FG-IR-26-267 and FG-IR-26-268, published on 10 November 2026, FortiOS and FortiSASE are affected by CVE-2026-126253, an authentication bypass in the OSPF daemon rated CVSS 9.3, and CVE-2026-126254, a numeric truncation error in the BGP daemon rated CVSS 9.6 that an authenticated iBGP neighbour could use to cause denial of service. The advisories say FortiSASE 26.3.1 was remediated in 26.3.2 with no customer action, list FortiSASE 25.1 to 26.2 as needing migration to a fixed release, and mark both flaws as not known to be exploited. Fortinet FortiGuard Labs (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)Fortinet FortiGuard Labs (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)
