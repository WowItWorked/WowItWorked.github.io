Why it matters to a bank

  • An AI agent gives a customer wrong or non-compliant information
  • Conversations contain personal and payment data
  • Customers cannot reach a person when they need one

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-10-07

    Automation Anywhere agreed to acquire Boost.ai, a Nordic Capital-owned conversational and voice AI company with customers in financial services, telecommunications and insurance, as reported by Dealroom News on 7 October 2026. Terms were not disclosed and closing was expected in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals. Dealroom News (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-06-23

    Banking-platform vendor Backbase announced on 23 June 2026 that it had acquired Kasisto, a conversational and agentic AI provider for banks, and folded its platform and New York team into the Backbase Banking OS. Terms were not disclosed. Backbase (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  3. 2026-05-04

    Sierra, which builds AI customer-service agents, raised US$950 million led by GV and Tiger Global at a US$15 billion valuation, eight months after a round at US$10 billion, as reported by SiliconANGLE on 4 May 2026. SiliconANGLE (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  4. 2026-04-23

    SoundHound AI agreed to acquire the enterprise messaging company LivePerson at an equity value of about US$43 million and an implied enterprise value of about US$250 million, as reported by FinTech Global on 23 April 2026. Closing was expected in the second half of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals. FinTech Global (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  5. 2025-09-08

    NICE announced on 8 September 2025 that it had closed its acquisition of Cognigy, a conversational and agentic AI company, after receiving regulatory approvals. When the deal was announced on 28 July 2025, NICE valued Cognigy at about US$955 million. Recent events

  1. 2026-11-18 · partnership NICE Ltd.CognigyMicrosoft
    NICE announces NiCE Cognigy integrations with Microsoft Teams, Azure and Dynamics 365

    NICE announced that NiCE Cognigy had completed certification as a voice agent for Microsoft Teams Phone, able to answer calls and transfer callers with context, and a new integration with Azure, Teams and Dynamics 365 Customer Service. The offerings were stated to be available on Microsoft Marketplace. NiCE press release (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-11-17 · launch Genesys
    Genesys launches Standalone AI Agents and Genesys Arena

    Genesys announced Standalone AI Agents, which let an organisation deploy the Genesys Cloud Agentic Virtual Agent without moving its contact centre to Genesys Cloud, together with Genesys Arena, a set of prebuilt agent kits, and an effort-based pricing model. Genesys (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  3. 2026-11-12 · launch Cognigy
    NICE launches a next generation of NiCE Cognigy with reusable skills and decision tracing

    NICE announced a new generation of NiCE Cognigy with a redesigned builder, agents assembled from reusable skills, tools and personas, an AI assistant for building and testing, tracing of each step an agent takes, and native speech-to-speech models. Existing deployments continue unchanged; default models are included in the conversation price and premium third-party models are billed on usage. NiCE Cognigy (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  4. 2026-11-05 · acquisition Sierra
    Sierra acquires browser-agent infrastructure company Anon

    Sierra announced that Anon, which built infrastructure for AI agents to sign in to and operate websites that have no API, is joining Sierra. Terms were not disclosed. Sierra said the technology will let its agents work in systems without APIs. Sierra (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  5. 2026-10-16 · security incident Genesys
    Genesys discloses unauthorised access to internal learning and CRM systems

    Genesys disclosed that on 2026-10-16 it became aware that an unauthorised third party had accessed internal business systems, including a learning management system and a customer relationship management platform, and that business contact and account information was accessed or downloaded. Genesys stated that the Genesys Cloud and Genesys Engage platforms and the customer data in them were not affected, and said on 2026-10-23 that the incident was contained with review continuing. Reported by SecurityWeek on 2026-10-26: a group calling itself ScarlettGroup claimed the attack. Genesys (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)SecurityWeek (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  6. 2026-07-16 · launch Sierra
    Sierra introduces Horizon for longer-running agent tasks

    Sierra published a post introducing Horizon on 2026-07-16. Its home page describes Horizon as covering long-horizon planning, customer context, outcome optimisation and proactive engagement, and Horizon has its own service-specific terms. Sierra (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)Sierra (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)Sierra (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  7. 2026-06-23 · acquisition Kasisto
    Backbase acquires Kasisto and folds its agentic AI platform into the Backbase Banking OS

    Backbase announced on 2026-06-23 that it had acquired Kasisto. Kasisto's agentic AI platform, financial-services intelligence and New York-based team became part of Backbase and its Banking OS.
