Why it matters to a bank

  • An intruder who is already inside moves between systems for days or weeks before any control notices
  • Stolen or misused credentials look like legitimate activity to controls that rely on signatures or behaviour baselines
  • Security operations staff lose time to low-confidence alerts and cannot tell quickly which ones are real

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-11-22

    Vendors in this market describe the same core idea: decoys, lures and fake credentials that no legitimate user should touch, so that any contact is treated as a confirmed signal. Zscaler sells deception as part of its wider zero trust platform and Fortinet sells FortiDeceptor as part of its security operations platform, including a hosted version, while Acalvio and CounterCraft are specialists. In 2026 all four present deception as a defence against attacks run by AI agents, which probe many paths at once and so are likely to touch a decoy. The decoys themselves are spreading to AI infrastructure: Zscaler lists decoy chatbots and language-model interfaces, Acalvio announced decoys placed in AI agent workflows in July 2026, and CounterCraft describes AI agents that build and run the deception environment. Coverage of operational technology and of identity stores such as Active Directory is now common to the vendors read.
