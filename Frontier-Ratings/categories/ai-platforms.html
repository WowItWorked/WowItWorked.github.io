Why it matters to a bank

  • AI is built on ungoverned tools outside the bank's controls
  • Applications are tied to one platform's proprietary services
  • Costs and usage are hard to attribute and control

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-06-30

    The Council of the EU gave final approval to the Digital Omnibus on AI, which moves the application date of the EU AI Act's high-risk rules to 2 December 2027 for stand-alone systems and 2 August 2028 for systems embedded in products, according to an EU External Action Service notice of 30 June 2026. The regulation enters into force on the third day after publication in the Official Journal. European External Action Service (regulator or standards body, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-06-25

    TrueFoundry, a start-up that manages AI workloads, acquired Seldon, an open-source model-deployment platform founded in 2014 whose enterprise customers include PayPal and Experian, as reported by SiliconANGLE on 25 June 2026. The price was not disclosed. SiliconANGLE (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  3. 2026-04-29

    Anaconda announced on 29 April 2026 the acquisition of Outerbounds, the company behind the open-source ML orchestration framework Metaflow, and said it would keep developing Metaflow as open source. Terms were not disclosed. Anaconda (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  4. 2025-02-10

    DataRobot announced on 10 February 2025 that it had acquired Agnostiq and its open-source compute-orchestration platform Covalent to support agentic AI application development. Terms were not disclosed. DataRobot (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  3. 2026-09-29 · outage Microsoft
    Azure OpenAI and Foundry services degraded in Sweden Central for about six hours

    Microsoft stated in a post-incident review that between 10:03 and 15:58 UTC on 29 September 2026 Azure OpenAI Service, Foundry Agent Service, Foundry Models and Azure AI services in the Sweden Central region returned intermittent failures, higher latency and HTTP 500 and 503 errors. Microsoft attributed this to a planned deployment that cleared internal caches at peak time and, with two caching defects, sent about 20 times normal traffic to a regional backend service. Microsoft (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)
