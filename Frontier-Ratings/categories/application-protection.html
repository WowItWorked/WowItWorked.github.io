Why it matters to a bank

  • The bank's mobile app is reverse engineered, modified or repackaged, and customers install a tampered copy
  • Malware on a customer's device uses overlays, accessibility services or screen capture to steal credentials or authorise payments
  • The bank's back end cannot tell a genuine copy of its app from a script, emulator or cloned app calling its APIs

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-11-23

    OWASP's mobile verification standard groups this job into four controls: checking the integrity of the platform, anti-tampering, and resistance to static and to dynamic analysis. Vendors describe the same core and add to it. Zimperium's suite combines app shielding, in-app runtime protection with a central console, white-box key protection and security testing. Digital.ai offers obfuscation, anti-tamper, runtime self-protection, threat monitoring and white-box cryptography across mobile, web and desktop apps. Promon applies protection after the app is compiled, with no code changes, and adds app integrity checks for APIs. Appdome automates protections in the build pipeline and feeds threat data back to the business. The direction is from build-time hardening alone towards a lifecycle: telemetry from apps in the field, server-side attestation so policies change without a new release, and AI-assisted or guided configuration. The market is also consolidating: Guardsquare completed its purchase of Verimatrix's XTD application protection assets in February 2026, after Verimatrix chose to concentrate on video protection.
