Why it matters to a bank

  • Staff are deceived by phishing, voice, message or deepfake impersonation into moving money or giving up credentials
  • Generic annual training does not prepare staff for personalised, AI-generated attacks
  • The bank cannot show auditors and supervisors who was trained, how staff performed, or where human risk is concentrated

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-11-22

    Vendors in this market now describe themselves as human risk management platforms and no longer only as training libraries. Hoxhunt simulates phishing by email, SMS, phone call and Microsoft Teams and generates training with AI; Proofpoint's ZenGuide scores risk for each user and enrols people in training automatically; SoSafe sells a human risk management tool and an AI chatbot beside its training and simulations. The market is also converging with email security: KnowBe4 lists inbound and outbound email security and AI agent products beside its awareness training, and Hoxhunt offers automated analysis of the emails staff report.
