Why it matters to a bank

  • Account takeover through stolen passwords, phishing and intercepted one-time codes causes direct customer loss
  • Weak recovery and device-change processes let an attacker re-enrol as the customer
  • Sign-in friction and separate logins per channel push customers away from digital services and towards the call centre

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-11-22

    Vendors' current material shows the category moving from passwords and one-time codes to passkeys and from sign-in alone to the whole customer journey. Okta's Auth0 documentation describes sign-in with passwords, social accounts or one-time codes, multi-factor authentication for sensitive data, single sign-on, user management and protection against credential attacks. Microsoft's Entra External ID lists self-service sign-up with custom attributes, email one-time passcodes, FIDO2 passkeys, self-service password reset, extra verification for sensitive actions after sign-in and extensions for a customer's own logic. Products aimed at banks add approval of payments: OneSpan describes passkeys whose approval is cryptographically bound to the amount and recipient, with device-bound credentials inside the banking app, and Transmit Security documents transaction signing with passkeys or mobile biometrics.
