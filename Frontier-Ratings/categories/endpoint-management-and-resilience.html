Why it matters to a bank

  • Devices the bank cannot see or reach are left unpatched and outside its security controls
  • Security agents and required software are removed, disabled or broken on endpoints without anyone noticing
  • A faulty update or an attack leaves many staff devices unusable and they cannot be recovered without hands-on work

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-11-23

    Vendors in this market describe a common core: a live inventory of devices and software, operating-system and third-party patching, policy and compliance enforcement, software deployment and remote troubleshooting from one console. Ivanti and NinjaOne both add automation that corrects endpoint problems without an operator, Tanium presents autonomous remediation with governed, AI-assisted actions, and Microsoft Intune has added an AI assistant and agents for policy and remediation work. Recovery is the newer theme: Absolute Security sells firmware-embedded persistence that reinstalls its agent and can restore a device whose operating system will not start, and in October 2026 it announced the purchase of the FirmGuard firmware security business from Phoenix Technologies. Endpoint management, patching and vulnerability remediation are being sold together, so this category overlaps with vulnerability and exposure management.
