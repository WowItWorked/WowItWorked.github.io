Why it matters to a bank

  • An expired or mis-issued certificate takes a payment, authentication or customer-facing service offline
  • Certificates and certificate authorities are spread across teams and suppliers with no single inventory, owner or policy
  • Shorter certificate lifetimes and the move to new algorithms cannot be met with manual renewal and without knowing which algorithms are in use

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-11-23

    Certificate lifecycle products converge on the same core. Keyfactor Command, DigiCert Trust Lifecycle Manager and Sectigo Certificate Manager each describe discovery of certificates into one inventory and automated issuance and renewal, and each says it takes in certificates from certificate authorities other than the vendor's own; Keyfactor and DigiCert also describe policy on algorithms, key sizes or lifetimes and role-based access. Two pressures are driving automation. The CA/Browser Forum adopted ballot SC-081v3 in April 2025, which reduces the maximum validity of public TLS certificates in steps from 398 days to 47 days between March 2026 and March 2029, and Keyfactor and DigiCert now present visibility of post-quantum certificates or migration to them as part of the product. A separate strand, described by PKI Solutions as posture management, monitors the certificate authorities, revocation services and hardware security modules themselves rather than issuing certificates, and is sold to run alongside a lifecycle tool.
