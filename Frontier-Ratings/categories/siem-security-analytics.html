Why it matters to a bank

  • Attacks go unnoticed because logs are not collected or correlated
  • Log volume makes the monitoring bill unpredictable
  • The bank cannot reconstruct events for a regulator after an incident

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-07-14

    Telemetry company Cribl bought detection-engineering vendor CardinalOps, taking its intellectual property and about 20 security engineers, for an undisclosed price. TechTarget reported it as Cribl's first move beyond data routing into detection content, which Cribl described as SIEM-like rather than a full SIEM replacement. TechTarget (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  2. 2025-05-27

    CISA, with the Australian Signals Directorate's Australian Cyber Security Centre and other partners, published guidance for organisations procuring and implementing SIEM and SOAR platforms: separate documents for executives and practitioners, plus practitioner guidance on which logs to prioritise for SIEM ingestion. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (regulator or standards body, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  3. 2024-09-04

    Palo Alto Networks closed its acquisition of IBM's QRadar software-as-a-service assets. The two companies said QRadar SaaS customers would be helped to move to Palo Alto's Cortex XSIAM, with no-cost migration services from IBM Consulting for eligible customers, while on-premises QRadar customers continue to get IBM support. Palo Alto Networks (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  4. 2024-07-17

    Exabeam and LogRhythm completed their merger; the combined company kept the Exabeam name. Exabeam (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  5. 2024-03-18

    Cisco completed its acquisition of Splunk for $157 per share in cash, about $28 billion in equity value. Cisco (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

Recent events

  1. 2026-11-20 · certification Dropzone AI
    Dropzone AI says its AI SOC Analyst is FedRAMP High (Class D) certified through Second Front's platform

    Dropzone AI said in a press release published on 2026-11-20 that its AI SOC Analyst had obtained FedRAMP High (Class D) certification through Second Front's 2F Game Warden platform, making it available to US federal agencies for production use. The release points to Second Front's FedRAMP Marketplace listing (FR2410866384) and names no authorising agency. That listing, checked on 2026-11-22, shows 2F Game Warden as FedRAMP Certified at Class D (High) since 2025-08-12 but does not name Dropzone AI, so the certification runs through Second Front's platform and Dropzone's inclusion rests on its own statement. Dropzone AI (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)FedRAMP (U.S. General Services Administration) (regulator or standards body, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-11-18 · launch Sumo Logic
    Sumo Logic releases AWS Observability Solution v3.0.0 with 20 regions and Marketplace deployment

    Sumo Logic announced on 2026-11-18 an expanded AWS Observability Solution that adds the Zurich, Melbourne and New Zealand regions, for 20 supported AWS regions, and can be bought and deployed through AWS Marketplace Quick Launch. The press release says version 3.0.0 is available to Sumo Logic customers now. The release concerns observability rather than the Cloud SIEM product. Sumo Logic (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  3. 2026-11-17 · launch Microsoft
    Microsoft makes Perception security agents generally available and folds Sentinel SIEM features into Defender

    In a Microsoft Security blog post dated 2026-11-17, Microsoft said Perception, a system that coordinates attack-simulation, detection and remediation agents, is generally available. It also said the core of an Integrated Security Operations Center in Defender is generally available, giving eligible Defender Suite, Microsoft 365 E5 and E7 customers Sentinel SIEM features and 90-day default retention for Defender data. Entra dynamic permissions and Conditional Access controls are in limited preview, and several Purview data-protection features for Copilot are due in preview or general availability in January 2027. No prices are listed. Microsoft (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  4. 2026-11-16 · launch Exaforce
    Exaforce introduces ExaU for building custom security AI agents

    Exaforce said in a blog post dated 16 November 2026 that ExaU lets security teams create their own AI agents, which it calls Exabots, with defined roles, persistent memory, scheduled routines and permission boundaries. The post says actions that change data need the user's approval and that ExaU is included in the Exaforce platform and available now; it gives no pricing. Exaforce (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  5. 2026-11-14 · launch Exaforce
    Exaforce introduces Force ER emergency incident response service

    Exaforce said in a blog post dated 14 November 2026 that it is introducing Force ER, an emergency incident response service open to organisations that are not existing customers and that needs no retainer. The post says a responder answers within 15 minutes and that Exaforce's managed detection and response team then works the incident on the Exaforce platform; it gives no pricing. Exaforce (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  6. 2026-10-16 · regulatory action Microsoft
    UK CMA proposes to designate Microsoft with strategic market status in business software

    The UK Competition and Markets Authority published on 2026-10-16 a proposed decision to designate Microsoft as having strategic market status in respect of its business software ecosystem, with a roadmap of possible interventions, and opened a consultation. The decision is provisional; no final designation was found. Competition and Markets Authority (regulator or standards body, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  7. 2026-10-15 · litigation Exabeam
    Proposed settlement notified in stockholder class action over Exabeam and LogRhythm merger

    According to the settlement website for Mihovilovic v. Exabeam, Inc. (Delaware Court of Chancery, C.A. No. 2024-0976-PAF), a proposed US$7,000,000 cash settlement of a stockholder class action over Exabeam's 2024 merger with LogRhythm awaits a court hearing in December 2026. A claims-notice site, ClaimDepot, published a notice on 15 October 2026 describing the fund as US$20.5M including equity and saying the defendants deny wrongdoing. The date recorded is that of the ClaimDepot notice; the date the settlement was agreed was not sourced. Settlement administrator for Mihovilovic v. Exabeam, Inc. (primary filing, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)ClaimDepot (other, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  8. 2026-10-08 · launch Elastic
    Elastic announces AlertZero AI agents for Elastic Security in technical preview

    Elastic announced AlertZero, a layer of specialised AI agents in Elastic Security organised into triage, hunt, detection and forensics 'Watches', with analysts setting how much each may do without approval. It is a technical preview for Elastic Cloud, self-managed and air-gapped deployments and works with a model of the customer's choice. No pricing was given.
