Why it matters to a bank

  • Security and incident teams have not practised against the attacks most likely to hit the bank, so response is slow or wrong when one happens
  • Executives and crisis teams meet their first major cyber incident without having rehearsed the decisions it demands
  • The bank cannot show its board and supervisors measured evidence that its people can detect, respond to and recover from a cyber attack

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-11-23

    Vendors in this market have moved from libraries of individual training labs to platforms that exercise whole teams and report a measure of readiness. Hack The Box sells labs, a threat range for incident drills, tabletop crisis exercises and a readiness index for each analyst, mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, NIST NICE and DORA. Cyberbit sells a cloud cyber range, team live-fire exercises, solo labs, an executive crisis simulation and its own readiness index. Immersive's product guide lists labs, cyber range exercises, live threat ranges, crisis simulations and peer benchmarking in one platform. SimSpace builds replicas of a customer's production environment for team rehearsal and for testing tools. The newest direction is testing AI agents alongside people: Hack The Box, Immersive and SimSpace each now describe assessing or validating AI agents on their ranges.
