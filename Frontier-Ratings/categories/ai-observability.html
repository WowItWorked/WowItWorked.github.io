Why it matters to a bank

  • The bank cannot show what its AI systems said to customers
  • Quality degrades silently after a model or prompt change
  • Evaluation is manual and not repeatable

    Fiddler AI and AMD describe joint approach to running monitored AI agents on AMD hardware

    A post on Fiddler AI's blog dated 16 November 2026, which says it first appeared on AMD's blog, describes a technical collaboration in which AMD supplies accelerated computing and open software and Fiddler supplies evaluation, observability and guardrails for agents running on it. The post mentions no investment or commercial terms. Fiddler AI (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-11-10 · launch Braintrust
    Braintrust releases a Terraform provider for managing its configuration as code

    Braintrust announced on its blog on 2026-11-10 a Terraform provider that manages access control, project setup and shared configuration such as AI secrets and automations as code, plus a command-line tool that exports an existing organisation's resources. Braintrust said the provider is published on the Terraform and OpenTofu registries. Braintrust (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  3. 2026-11-10 · launch Fiddler AI
    Fiddler AI introduces Agent Registry, an inventory of AI agents in its control plane

    Fiddler AI said in a blog post dated 10 November 2026 that it is introducing Agent Registry, a central inventory in the Fiddler AI Control Plane that lists each agent with its model, tools and deployment context alongside alerts and token usage. The post does not say whether the feature is generally available or in preview. Fiddler AI (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  4. 2026-11-03 · acquisition CrowdStrike
    CrowdStrike signs definitive agreement to acquire Fiddler AI; deal not yet closed

    CrowdStrike announced in a press release dated 2026-11-03 that it had signed a definitive agreement to acquire Fiddler AI, an AI observability vendor. CrowdStrike said Fiddler's product will be sold within the Falcon platform as Falcon Observe, with general availability expected in its fourth quarter of fiscal 2027, that the price will be paid mostly in cash with a portion in stock subject to vesting, and that closing is expected in the same quarter subject to customary conditions. No price was disclosed. The same agreement is also recorded from Fiddler's side as fiddler-ai-evt-2026-11-crowdstrike-agreement. CrowdStrike (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

    Dynatrace completes its acquisition of Arize AI

    Dynatrace announced on 2026-10-01 that it had completed its acquisition of Arize.
