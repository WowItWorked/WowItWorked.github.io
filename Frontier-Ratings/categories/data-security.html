Why it matters to a bank

  • The bank does not know where all copies of customer data are
  • Excessive access to sensitive data goes unnoticed
  • Data leaks through email, cloud storage or AI tools

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-10-16

    Proofpoint, owned by Thoma Bravo, agreed to acquire Varonis for $49.75 per share in cash, about $6.3 billion, with closing expected in the first half of 2027. SecurityWeek described it as the largest cybersecurity acquisition announced so far in 2026; Proofpoint plans to combine the two into a single data and AI security platform. SecurityWeek (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-07-28

    Cyera agreed to acquire Oasis Security, a provider of non-human identity and agentic access governance, for about $1 billion, saying it would bring identity and data security into one platform as use of AI agents grows. SecurityWeek (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

    Cyera raised $600 million at a $12 billion valuation in a round led by Evolution Equity Partners, taking its total funding above $2.3 billion. SecurityWeek (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

    Veeam, a data-resilience vendor owned by Insight Partners, agreed to buy data security company Securiti AI for $1.725 billion in cash and stock, with closing expected in the first week of December 2025. TechCrunch (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

Recent events

  1. 2026-11-20 · launch Concentric AI
    Concentric AI announces an endpoint agent for Semantic DLP

    Concentric AI announced the Semantic DLP Agent, extending its controls from the browser to desktop AI applications, coding agents and locally run models, with analysis of whether a prompt fits the user's role. Concentric AI (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-23, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-11-19 · launch MIND
    MIND adds email data loss prevention for Microsoft Exchange

    MIND announced an email module that classifies message and attachment content against the recipient and can block outbound Microsoft Exchange mail before delivery, using the same policies as its other channels. MIND (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-21, opens in new tab)

  3. 2026-11-18 · launch MIND
    MIND makes Microsoft 365 and Copilot integrations generally available

    MIND announced generally available integrations with Microsoft Copilot, Purview, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Edge and Exchange, including reading and applying Purview sensitivity labels and a Windows endpoint agent, and said the product can be bought through the Microsoft Marketplace. MIND (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-21, opens in new tab)

  4. 2026-11-16 · acquisition Varonis Systems
    Varonis sets 22 December stockholder vote on US$6.3B all-cash sale to Proofpoint

    Varonis filed a definitive merger proxy with the SEC on 2026-11-16 calling a virtual special meeting for 2026-12-22 (record date 2026-11-16) to vote on its sale to Proofpoint, a Thoma Bravo portfolio company, for US$49.75 per share in cash. The deal, announced in a joint release filed on 2026-10-15 and valued at about US$6.3B, is expected to close in the first half of 2027 subject to stockholder and regulatory approvals; Varonis would then leave Nasdaq and become private. The proxy says US antitrust notifications were filed on 2026-10-19 and that clearances in five other jurisdictions are also required. US Securities and Exchange Commission (EDGAR) (primary filing, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)US Securities and Exchange Commission (EDGAR) (primary filing, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  5. 2026-11-12 · partnership BigID
    DataHub releases connector bringing BigID classifications into its catalog

    DataHub announced in a Business Wire release on 2026-11-12 a partnership with BigID and a connector that copies BigID's data classification and sensitivity findings onto the matching tables and columns in the DataHub catalog. DataHub said the connector is available in its open-source and cloud editions, reads from BigID on a schedule, and that the two companies are exploring two-way synchronisation. DataHub (Business Wire via FinancialContent) (other, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  6. 2026-11-11 · launch Varonis Systems
    Varonis adds LLM Gateway and MCP Gateway to Atlas AI Security

    The Varonis changelog records the release of an LLM Gateway for controlling AI model usage and spend and an MCP Gateway for governing agent tool access, both within Atlas AI Security, followed on 12 November by an Agent Manifest feature. Varonis (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  7. 2026-11-10 · launch Cyera
    Cyera announces Cy Agents and Cyera Endpoint

    At its datasecAI 2026 event Cyera announced Cy Agents, a set of AI agents for detection, triage and investigation, and Cyera Endpoint, an on-device sensor that extends data loss prevention to laptops and workstations. The release does not say whether either is generally available. Cyera (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  8. 2026-11-05 · launch Cyberhaven
    Cyberhaven puts Agentic Data Security module on sale

    Cyberhaven announced that Agentic Data Security, a Flow module that finds AI agents, records their prompts, tool calls and file reads, and applies data policies to their actions, was available for purchase. Cyberhaven (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-21, opens in new tab)
