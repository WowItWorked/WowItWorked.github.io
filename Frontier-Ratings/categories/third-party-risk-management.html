Why it matters to a bank

  • A supplier's breach or outage becomes the bank's incident
  • Supervisors expect a complete register of third-party arrangements
  • Assessments are point-in-time and go stale

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-05-14

    SecurityScorecard acquired UK internet-scanning and threat intelligence startup Driftnet for an undisclosed price, as reported by SiliconANGLE, which noted the company had earlier bought vendor security review automation firm HyperComply. SiliconANGLE (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  2. 2025-11-18

    The three European Supervisory Authorities (EBA, EIOPA and ESMA) designated critical ICT third-party providers under the Digital Operational Resilience Act, drawing on data from financial entities' registers of information. The designated providers come under direct ESA oversight of their risk management and governance. European Banking Authority (regulator or standards body, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  3. 2024-11-14

    Bitsight, a cyber risk management company, is buying Israeli dark-web threat intelligence firm Cybersixgill for $115 million, as reported by TechCrunch on 14 November 2024. TechCrunch (independent press, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  4. 2024-11-12

    The Bank of England, PRA and FCA published final rules for critical third parties to the UK financial sector (PS16/24), taking effect on 1 January 2025 and applying to a provider once HM Treasury's designation order for it comes into force; none had been designated at publication. The regulators state that the regime does not reduce firms' own responsibility for managing risks from their third-party arrangements. Bank of England (regulator or standards body, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  5. 2023-06-06

    The OCC, the Federal Reserve Board and the FDIC issued joint guidance on managing risk in third-party relationships (OCC Bulletin 2023-17). It sets out principles for each stage of the third-party risk management life cycle, aligns supervisory expectations across the three agencies, and rescinds OCC Bulletin 2013-29 and its 2020 FAQ. Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (regulator or standards body, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  1. 2026-11-19 · acquisition UpGuard
    Novacoast acquires UpGuard's managed vendor assessment business

    UpGuard announced that Novacoast acquired its managed vendor assessments service business, with the delivering analysts moving to Novacoast from 2026-11-15. Customers continue to use the UpGuard platform. Terms were not disclosed. UpGuard (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-20, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-11-18 · launch OneTrust
    OneTrust announces Fall '26 Release with AI model intelligence, lineage views and AI-run third-party research

    OneTrust announced its Fall '26 Release on 18 November 2026. For AI Governance it adds a curated library of third-party foundation-model information in the AI inventory, an interactive lineage view across systems, models, agents and controls, and usage and risk dashboards. For Third-Party Management it lets a workflow rule trigger an AI research skill that gathers public information and drafts questionnaire answers, and widens AI document scanning. The release gives no availability date or packaging details. OneTrust (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  3. 2026-11-12 · launch Lema AI

    Lema's blog, in a post its blog index dates 2026-11-12, announced Outreach Campaigns, a feature for sending a single questionnaire to many vendors at once and tracking the responses outside regular assessments, aimed at urgent situations such as a newly disclosed vulnerability. The company says it is available to all Lema customers. Lema AI (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)
