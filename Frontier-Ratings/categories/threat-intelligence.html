Why it matters to a bank

  • The bank learns that it or its sector is being targeted only once an attack is under way
  • Stolen credentials, planned attacks or data offered for sale in criminal forums go unseen because the bank has no view of those sources
  • Analysts cannot tell which of thousands of indicators and alerts matter, so effort goes to noise while relevant threats wait

Where the category is heading

  1. 2026-11-22

    Providers in this market describe a common core: collection from closed criminal forums, marketplaces and messaging channels alongside open and technical sources, analyst-written reporting, alerting tailored to the customer, and delivery into security tools. Flashpoint and Intel 471 both add AI assistance for summarising and reporting; Intel 471 ties collection to the customer's stated intelligence requirements and generates ready-to-run queries for detection tools. Vendors are moving from supplying intelligence to acting on it: Recorded Future launched Autonomous Threat Operations in October 2025 to run threat hunting continuously across a customer's security tools. Ownership is consolidating into larger groups: Mastercard reported acquiring Recorded Future in December 2024 and launching a payment-focused threat intelligence service built on it in 2025.   5. 2026-06-12 · security incident Recorded Future
    Recorded Future discloses exposure of sales-system contact data through a compromise at supplier Klue

    Disclosed by Recorded Future in a blog post in June 2026: Klue, a marketing supplier, reported unauthorised access on 12 June 2026 that reached Recorded Future's Salesforce account through a compromised integration token, which Recorded Future confirmed on 17 June. The company said the data was believed to be limited to business contact details and possibly some contract information, that its investigation was continuing, and that it had found no evidence its own systems, the Intelligence Graph or customer platform data were accessed. It said it revoked the tokens, reviewed other integrations and contacted law enforcement. Recorded Future (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)
