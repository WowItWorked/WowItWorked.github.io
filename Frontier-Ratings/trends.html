How to read this

  • Added means the record first appears in that version. Removed means it was in the version before and is no longer tracked.
  • A score change is listed whenever the one-decimal score for any of the seven dimensions differs from the version before. The reasoning shown is the evaluator’s reasoning for the new score.
  • Scores are 0 to 5. “No public evidence” means nothing could be established from public sources; it is never counted as zero.
  • The method, including the validation that can hold a score change back, is on the methodology page.
