Identity verification and risk platform combining ID document checks, selfie matching and liveness, government database and address checks, AML screening, risk signals and a rules-based orchestration layer. How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (23)

Each is something that could not be established from public sources. Export them as a list to send, with empty columns for the vendor’s answer and the evidence supplied.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. In what year was Jumio founded, and when was the current legal entity incorporated?
    Company age is an input to maturity and viability.
  2. How many full-time employees does Jumio have, and how many work in engineering, fraud operations and customer support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a large bank deployment.
  3. How much equity capital has Jumio raised in total, and what share of the most recent round was primary capital?
    Total capital raised is an input to judging financial resilience.
  4. Has Jumio reduced headcount in the last 24 months, by how much, and in which functions?
    Headcount reductions can reduce support and product capacity.
  5. Has Jumio had any security incident affecting customer or end-user data in the last five years, and how was each disclosed and remediated?
    A security incident at an identity verification provider can expose identity documents and biometric data.
  6. Has Jumio lost any customer accounting for a material share of revenue in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of major customers can signal product or trust problems.
  7. What logs does the Jumio Platform make available to the customer, how long are they retained, and how can they be retrieved?
    A bank needs logs of what the service did with each verification to investigate disputes and incidents.
  8. Does the Jumio Platform keep an audit trail of user actions and configuration changes, how long is it retained, and can it be exported?
    An audit trail of configuration changes and reviewer actions is needed for internal audit and model governance.
Show the other 15
  1. By what mechanisms (API, webhooks, streaming, file export) can a customer export decision, event and log data from the Jumio Platform?
    Export of decision and event data lets the bank monitor the service in its own tools.
  2. Which SIEM products does the Jumio Platform integrate with, and by what mechanism are logs delivered?
    Security teams need vendor activity logs in their SIEM.
  3. What reports or evidence packs can the Jumio Platform produce for auditors and regulators, and can the vendor share a sample?
    Auditors and supervisors ask for evidence of how identity decisions were made.
  4. In which regions can a customer's data in the Jumio Platform be stored and processed, and can the customer choose and restrict the region?
    Identity documents and biometrics are subject to data-localisation and transfer rules.
  5. Can Jumio provide the current list of sub-processors for the Jumio Platform, with the purpose and processing location of each, and its notice period for changes?
    A bank must know every party that handles its customers' identity data.
  6. Is data submitted through the Jumio Platform used to train or improve models that serve other customers, on what legal basis, and can a customer opt out by contract?
    Use of customers' identity documents and biometrics to train shared models is a data-protection and consent question for the bank.
  7. How does the Jumio Platform encrypt customer data in transit and at rest, and how are keys managed and rotated?
    Encryption in transit and at rest is a baseline control for identity data.
  8. Does the Jumio Platform support customer-managed encryption keys, and for which data stores?
    Customer-managed keys let a bank revoke access to its data.
  9. Has Jumio had any breach of customer or end-user personal data, and can it provide the notifications it issued?
    A history of data breaches is a direct input to data risk.
  10. Can Jumio provide its incident and uptime history for the Jumio Platform for the last 24 months, with post-incident reviews?
    Outage history indicates how often onboarding could be interrupted.
  11. Does the Jumio Platform rely on any third-party AI or biometric model providers, and which ones?
    Reliance on third-party AI models is a fourth-party dependency.
  12. On termination, how can a customer export its verification records from the Jumio Platform, in what formats, and when and how is data deleted?
    A bank must be able to leave and to retrieve or delete its data.
  13. From which countries do Jumio support and review staff access customer data, and can access be restricted to chosen jurisdictions?
    Support staff with access to identity data may sit in other jurisdictions.
  14. What logs, audit trail and export mechanisms does the Jumio Platform give a customer, and does it integrate with any SIEM?
    Observability cannot be scored without evidence of logs, audit trail and export.
  15. Where is data in the Jumio Platform stored and processed, from where do support staff access it, and can a customer choose the region?
    Country risk cannot be scored without processing and support locations.
