Microsegmentation product, formerly Guardicore Centra, that maps communications between workloads at process level and enforces label-based policy with agents or agentless methods across data centres, clouds, Kubernetes, endpoints and legacy systems. How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Open as a question list
  1. How many customers run Akamai Guardicore Segmentation in production today, and how many are banks?

  2. Which shareholders hold 5% or more of Akamai's common stock, and where are they domiciled?
    Large holders can influence strategy and a change of control.
  3. Has Akamai been subject to any regulatory investigation, order or penalty in the last five years, and what was the outcome?
    Regulatory action against a security vendor bears on a bank's own supervisory standing.
  4. Has Akamai lost any large financial-services customer of Guardicore Segmentation in the last three years, and why?
    Loss of large customers can signal product or service problems.
  5. Have there been any departures of senior executives or governance concerns at Akamai in the last three years?
    Leadership instability or misconduct affects delivery and governance.
  6. What is the price of Akamai Guardicore Segmentation per workload or asset, for SaaS and on-premises management, and what are the support fees?
    Without list prices a bank cannot compare cost before engaging sales.
  7. Which sub-processors handle customer data for Akamai Guardicore Segmentation, for what purpose and in which countries?
    A bank must know which third parties handle its data.
  8. Is customer traffic, process or label data used to train the AI features of Akamai Guardicore Segmentation, and are those models shared across customers?
    A bank needs to know whether its traffic and label data is used to train models shared with other customers.
Show the other 7

    Customer-managed keys let a bank control access to its data held by the vendor.
