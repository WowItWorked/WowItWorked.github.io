Fraud and financial-crime prevention platform for retail banks built on behavioural biometrics. An SDK collects behavioural, device, network, application and transaction signals from digital banking sessions; models score sessions for account opening fraud, account takeover, social-engineering scams and mule accounts. It includes investigation tools (Analyst Station, BioCatch Link, Rule Manager, Insights Query Engine) and BioCatch Trust, an inter-bank intelligence-sharing network.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Open as a question list

  2. When was BioCatch's most recent primary financing round, at what stage and for what amount?
    The date and size of the last financing indicate how long the vendor can fund operations.
  3. Has BioCatch reduced headcount in the last 24 months, and by how much?
    Headcount reductions can reduce support and product capacity.
  4. Has BioCatch had any security incident affecting customer data or service in the last five years, and how was each disclosed?
    A bank needs the vendor's security incident record before sending it customer data.
  5. Which financial-institution customers have ended their use of BioCatch in the last three years, and why?
    Loss of large customers can signal product or service problems.
  6. Have there been any unplanned executive departures or conduct findings at BioCatch in the last three years?
    Leadership instability or conduct issues affect vendor reliability.
  7. Which deployment models does BioCatch offer for BioCatch Connect (vendor-hosted multi-tenant, single-tenant, customer cloud, on-premises), and which do bank customers use?
    Deployment model determines where bank data sits and who operates the system.

    The bank needs system and user activity logs to monitor the service.
