CrewAI AMP (Agent Management Platform) is the commercial platform around the open-source CrewAI agent framework. Teams build multi-agent 'crews' and event-driven 'flows' in code or in a no-code studio, deploy them as automations, and operate them with traces, triggers, role-based access, a secrets manager and organisation-wide policies. It launched in October 2024 as CrewAI Enterprise. It is offered in CrewAI's cloud and, on the Enterprise plan, in the customer's own VPC or infrastructure.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

  1. 2026-03-30 · vulnerability disclosure CrewAI
    CERT/CC publishes four vulnerabilities in the CrewAI framework

    CERT/CC published vulnerability note VU#221883 on 2026-03-30, reporting four vulnerabilities in the open-source CrewAI framework (CVE-2026-2275, -2285, -2286 and -2287) covering code execution through the code interpreter tool's sandbox fallback, server-side request forgery in RAG search tools and local file reads in the JSON loader. SecurityWeek reported on 2026-03-31, and GBHackers on 2026-04-01, that no complete patch was available at publication. The note as revised on 2026-05-20 carries a vendor statement that the code interpreter tool was removed, path and URL validation were added, and all issues are fixed in current releases. This is a product vulnerability disclosure, not a reported breach of the vendor's systems.
