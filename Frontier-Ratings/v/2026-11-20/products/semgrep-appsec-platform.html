Code security platform built around the Semgrep static-analysis engine. It comprises Semgrep Code (static analysis), Semgrep Supply Chain (dependency analysis and malware blocking), Semgrep Secrets, and AI features under the Multimodal name for detection, triage and fix generation, plus Guardian for AI-generated code. Scans run in the customer's CI or as managed scans, with results held in the vendor's SaaS platform.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

CASTLE benchmark for static analysers and LLMs · CASTLE v2 preprint (arXiv 2503.09433v2)

Eotvos Lorand University and co-authors (Dubniczky et al.) · published 2025-03-31

Academic micro-benchmark of 250 small C programs covering 25 CWE classes, run by university researchers (not a recurring programme). Semgrep Code 1.110.0 placed 4th of 13 static analysers with a CASTLE Score of 541: 36 true positives, 70 false positives and 120 misses (precision 34%, recall 23%). CodeQL led the static analysers on 634. The test covers C only. arXiv (Dubniczky et al., Eotvos Lorand University and others) (other, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

What it measures: Scores static analysers, formal verifiers and language models on finding planted weaknesses in 250 small C programs covering 25 CWE classes. Who pays: Funded by the tester; vendors do not pay.
