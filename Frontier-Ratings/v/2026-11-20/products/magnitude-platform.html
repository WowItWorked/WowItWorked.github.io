Named customers

Organisations named in public sources as using this product, strongest evidence first. This is who has been confirmed, not a complete customer list.

No named customer could be confirmed from public sources. Vendors often cannot name bank customers, so this is not a finding about adoption.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (30)

Each is something that could not be established from public sources. Export them as a list to send, with empty columns for the vendor’s answer and the evidence supplied.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. In what year was Magnitude founded, and under what legal name?
    Company age is one input to maturity and viability.
  2. How many people does Magnitude employ, and how many work on Magnitude?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support and develop the product.
  3. What is Magnitude's current cash position and runway, and is the business profitable?
    A bank needs assurance the vendor can fund operations for the life of the contract.
  4. Has Magnitude reduced its workforce in the last 24 months, and did any reduction affect the teams that build or support Magnitude?
    Workforce reductions can affect support and product development.
  5. Has Magnitude been subject to any regulatory investigation, enforcement action, fine or order in the last five years?
    Regulatory action against a supplier can become a supervisory question for the bank.
  6. Is Magnitude party to any material litigation, and what is its status?
    Material litigation can affect a vendor's finances and focus.
  7. Has Magnitude had any security incident in the last five years that affected customer data or service, and what was done about it?
    A supplier's security incident can become the bank's incident.
  8. Has Magnitude lost any significant financial-services customers for Magnitude in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of significant customers can signal product or service problems.
Show the other 22
  1. Have there been any departures, disputes or conduct matters involving Magnitude's senior leadership in the last 24 months?
    Leadership instability can affect strategy and delivery.
  2. Which deployment models does Magnitude offer for Magnitude: multi-tenant SaaS, single-tenant, the customer's own cloud, or on premises?
    Deployment model determines where the bank's data sits and who operates the service.
  3. Can Magnitude produce the third-party registers supervisors require, such as the DORA register of information?
    Supervisors require complete registers of third-party arrangements.
  4. Does Magnitude offer a shared exchange where a supplier can publish evidence once for many customers?
    A shared exchange reduces the assessment burden on suppliers and speeds onboarding.
  5. What activity does Magnitude log, and can the customer view those logs?
    The bank needs logs of what the product and its users did.
  6. Can logs and audit records be exported from Magnitude by API or streaming, and in what format?
    Exportable telemetry lets the bank monitor the service in its own tools.
  7. Which SIEM products does Magnitude integrate with, and is the integration maintained by Magnitude?
    SIEM integration lets the bank's security operations monitor the product.
  8. What reports or evidence packs can Magnitude produce for the bank's auditors and regulators?
    Auditors and supervisors expect evidence the bank can hand over.
  9. In which regions can a customer's data for Magnitude be stored, and can the customer choose the region?
    Banks must know and often choose where their data is stored.
  10. Can Magnitude provide its current list of sub-processors for Magnitude, with the purpose and location of each?
    Sub-processors are fourth parties the bank must record and assess.
  11. How is customer data in Magnitude encrypted in transit and at rest, and with which algorithms and key lengths?
    Encryption in transit and at rest is a baseline control.
  12. Does Magnitude support customer-managed encryption keys?
    Customer-managed keys let a bank revoke access to its data.
  13. Has Magnitude had any breach affecting data held in or for Magnitude, and what was the outcome?
    Past breaches indicate how the vendor protects and responds.
  14. What service incidents has Magnitude had in the last 24 months, and is there a public status page?
    Outage history indicates reliability.
  15. Which cloud providers host Magnitude, and in which regions?
    Hosting providers are fourth parties and drive concentration risk.
  16. Which third-party AI model providers does Magnitude use, and what customer data is sent to them?
    Third-party model providers are fourth parties that may process the bank's data.
  17. What support hours, severity levels and response targets does Magnitude commit to for Magnitude?
    Support hours and response targets affect incident recovery.
  18. On termination, in what format and within what period will Magnitude return the customer's data from Magnitude, and when is it deleted?
    Supervisors expect a tested exit plan for material suppliers.
  19. In which countries is customer data for Magnitude processed?
    Processing locations determine legal exposure and country risk.
  20. From which countries do support staff access customer environments for Magnitude?
    Support staff may access customer data from other jurisdictions.
  21. What logs and audit records does Magnitude make available to the customer, can they be exported by API or to a SIEM, and how long are they retained?
    Observability cannot be placed on the rubric without knowing what logs and audit records the customer can access and export.
  22. In which countries is customer data for Magnitude processed and supported, and can the customer restrict both?
    Country risk cannot be scored without knowing where data is processed and from where support is given.
