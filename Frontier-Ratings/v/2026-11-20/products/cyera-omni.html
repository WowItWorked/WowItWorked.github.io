Data and AI security platform that discovers and classifies data across cloud, SaaS and on-premises stores, scores exposure, maps human and non-human identities to the data they can reach, adds a decision layer over existing data loss prevention tools, and inspects prompts sent to AI tools through a browser extension and an API. The vendor's November 2026 release calls the whole platform Cyera Omni; product pages name its parts DSPM, Omni DLP, Agent Guardian, Identity, Privacy and Endpoint.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. In what year, and in which jurisdiction, was Cyera founded and incorporated?
    Company age is an input to maturity and viability.
  2. Where is Cyera's headquarters, which legal entity contracts with customers, and where are Cyera Ltd. and Cyera US Inc. incorporated?
    Headquarters jurisdiction determines the legal regime the vendor and its contracts sit under.
  3. Has Cyera reduced headcount in the last 24 months, including after the Oasis Security, Otterize or other acquisitions?
    Layoffs can reduce support and product capacity.
  4. Has Cyera had any security incident affecting its corporate systems or platform, and how were customers notified?
    A security vendor's own incident record indicates how well it protects the access it is given to bank data.
  5. Which customers of more than US$1 million a year have not renewed in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of major customers can signal product or service problems.
  6. Have any founders or executives left Cyera in the last 24 months, and are there any governance disputes?
    Leadership disputes or departures can affect delivery and governance.
  7. How does Cyera explain an individual classification result or severity score, and can the explanation be exported?
    A bank needs to understand why data was classified or a risk scored in order to act on it and defend the decision.
  8. In which regions can a Cyera SaaS tenant be hosted, and what data leaves the customer's environment in each deployment model?
    A bank must know in which jurisdictions its data and metadata are stored.
Show the other 12
  1. Will Cyera provide its third-party and affiliate sub-processor lists, with purposes and locations, and its notice period for changes?
    Sub-processors are fourth parties with access to bank data.
  2. Does Cyera use customer data, metadata or classification results to train or tune models, and are any such models shared across customers?
    Use of bank data to train models shared across customers can leak information and breach confidentiality duties.
  3. How is customer data encrypted in transit and at rest in Cyera's SaaS service, and with what algorithms and key management?
    Encryption in transit and at rest is a baseline control for bank data.
  4. Can a customer supply and control its own encryption keys for data Cyera holds?
    Control of encryption keys limits vendor and cloud-provider access to bank data.
  5. Has customer data held or accessed by Cyera ever been exposed without authorisation?
    A past breach of customer data is directly relevant to the risk of giving the vendor access to bank data stores.
  6. Does Cyera run a status page customers can read, and what incidents affected the SaaS service in the last 24 months?
    Outage history shows how reliable the service is and how openly the vendor reports problems.
  7. Which model providers and models process customer data in Cyera's classification, Ask Cy and Cy Agents features, and can a customer restrict or choose them?
    Model providers are fourth parties that may process bank data.
  8. What availability commitment, service credits and support response targets does Cyera offer?
    Support hours, response targets and availability commitments determine how quickly a bank can recover from a fault.
  9. What data export formats, deletion commitments and exit assistance does Cyera provide at termination?
    A bank must be able to leave with its data and without disruption.
  10. In which countries is customer data processed by Cyera, its affiliates and its sub-processors?
    Processing locations drive data transfer and country risk assessments.
  11. From which countries do Cyera support and engineering staff access customer environments, and can access be restricted to chosen jurisdictions?
    Where support staff sit determines which jurisdictions can reach bank data during troubleshooting.
  12. Where are Cyera's headquarters, contracting entities, data processing locations and support teams, and can a customer choose the processing region?
    Country risk depends on where the vendor is based, who owns it and where data and support sit.
