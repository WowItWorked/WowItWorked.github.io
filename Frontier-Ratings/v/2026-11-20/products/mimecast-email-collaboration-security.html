Cloud email security sold in Core, Pro and Max tiers. Core covers attachment sandboxing, link and credential-phishing protection using computer vision, business email compromise detection and detection of compromised accounts; Pro adds mailbox backup, email continuity, secure messaging and browser isolation; Max adds scanning of Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive. It can be deployed as a mail gateway or, since March 2026, by API to Microsoft 365. DMARC management, awareness training and managed triage of reported email are separate Mimecast products.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. Has Mimecast reduced headcount in the last 24 months, and by how much?
    Layoffs can reduce support and product capacity.
  2. Has Mimecast lost any financial-services customers with more than 10,000 mailboxes in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of large customers can signal product or service problems.
  3. What prompted the change of chief executive in June 2026, and have any executives or directors left in the last 24 months in connection with a dispute or investigation?
    Leadership instability or misconduct affects vendor reliability.
  4. What message, detection and administrator logs does the product keep, for how long, and how can they be exported?
    Banks need message and administrative logs to investigate incidents.
  5. Does the product keep a tamper-evident audit trail of administrator and policy changes, and what is its retention?
    An audit trail of administrator actions is needed for control testing.
  6. Is our data used to train Mimecast's non-generative detection models, are those models shared across customers, and can we opt out?
    A bank must know whether its email content is used to train models that serve other customers.
  7. What encryption does Mimecast apply to customer data in transit and at rest, and where is this documented?
    Encryption in transit and at rest is a baseline control for email content.
  8. Does Mimecast support customer-managed or customer-held encryption keys for stored email data?
    Key control determines whether the bank can revoke the vendor's access to its data.
Show the other 3
  1. Can Mimecast provide its incident and availability history by grid for the last 24 months, with post-incident reviews?
    Outage history indicates how reliable the mail path is.
  2. Which third-party model providers does the email security product call, for which features, and what customer data is sent to them?
    Model providers are fourth parties whose outages or policy changes can affect the product.
  3. Can Mimecast provide documentation of its message logs, administrator audit trail, retention settings and SIEM export for the email security product?
    Observability cannot be scored without documentation of logs and audit trails.
