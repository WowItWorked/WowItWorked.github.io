How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. How many full-time employees does Glean have, and how many work in engineering, security and customer support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a large bank deployment.
  2. How much equity funding has Glean raised in total since founding, and how much cash does it hold?
    Total capital raised is an input to judging runway.
  3. Has Glean reduced headcount in the last 24 months, and if so by how much and in which functions?
    Layoffs can signal financial stress or reduced support capacity.
  4. Has Glean had any security incident affecting customer data or service in the last five years, and how was each disclosed?
    Past incidents and how they were handled indicate security maturity.
  5. Have any large or regulated customers stopped using Glean in the last two years, and why?
    Departures of large customers can signal product or commercial problems.
  6. Have there been senior leadership departures, investigations or governance issues at Glean in the last two years?
    Leadership instability or misconduct can affect a vendor's direction.
  7. What are Glean's per-seat and consumption prices, minimum commitments and the charges for the customer-hosted option?
    Banks need to budget and compare costs.
  8. Does Glean support customer-managed encryption keys in the Glean-hosted service and in customer-hosted deployments, and for which data stores?
    Key control determines whether the bank can revoke a vendor's access to its data.
