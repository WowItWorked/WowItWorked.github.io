Pegasystems' workflow automation and decisioning platform, whose current release is Pega Infinity '26. For agents it provides Pega Blueprint, which designs workflows and agents from documents and legacy systems; agents that run as governed steps inside workflows; Pega Agentic Process Fabric, a registry and orchestrator for Pega and third-party agents; and support for the MCP and A2A protocols. It is delivered as Pega Cloud or run by the client in its own cloud. No product launch date was sourced, so the ownership start follows the convention of the founding year; the agent features date from 2025.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (15)

Each is something that could not be established from public sources. Export them as a list to send, with empty columns for the vendor’s answer and the evidence supplied.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. How many organisations run Pega Platform in production, and how many of them are banks?
    A customer count shows how widely the platform is used.
  2. Has Pegasystems been subject to any regulatory investigation, order or penalty in the last five years, and what was the outcome?
    Regulatory action against a workflow supplier bears on a bank's own supervisory standing.
  3. Have any large banking customers moved off Pega Platform in the last two years, and why?
    Loss of large customers can signal product or service problems.
  4. What changes have there been to Pegasystems' executive team in the last two years, and were any connected to a dispute or investigation?
    Leadership instability affects delivery of the roadmap a bank depends on.
  5. What tools does Pega Platform provide to test and evaluate agents before release and to monitor their quality afterwards?
    Agents need testing before and after release to catch unsafe behaviour.
  6. How can audit records of agent actions in Pega Platform be exported or streamed to our tools, and how long are they kept?
    A bank needs agent and workflow audit records in its own monitoring and retention systems.
  7. Which SIEM products can Pega Cloud stream security and audit logs to?
    Security teams need platform and agent activity in their monitoring tools.
  8. Which sub-processors handle client data in Pega Cloud, including for generative AI and agent features, and where are they?
    A bank must know every party that can touch its data.
Show the other 7
  1. Is client data or prompt content used to train or tune any model, by Pegasystems or by its model providers, and is any such model shared across clients?
    A bank needs to know whether its data improves models used by others.
  2. How is client data encrypted at rest and in transit in Pega Cloud, and with which algorithms?
    Encryption at rest and in transit is a baseline control.
  3. What incidents affected Pega Cloud in the last 12 months, and what was the measured availability?
    Outage history shows whether the platform can carry critical bank operations.
  4. Which model providers serve Pega's generative AI and agent features in Pega Cloud, and can we restrict or replace them?
    Model providers are fourth parties that see prompt content.
  5. On termination, in what form can we export our applications, agent definitions and case data, and what help does Pegasystems give?
    Workflows and agents built on a proprietary platform are hard to move.
  6. In which regions can Pega Cloud host our environment, where do generative AI requests go, and can we fix both?
