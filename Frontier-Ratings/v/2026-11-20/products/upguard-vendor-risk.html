Third-party risk product on UpGuard's cyber risk posture management platform. It provides outside-in security ratings refreshed from daily scans, a questionnaire library and builder with AI-assisted assessment reports, alerts on changes in vendor posture, a Fourth Parties module on higher plans, and reporting. The companion Trust Exchange product lets a company publish its own questionnaires and security documents to customers. Sold in five plans by number of monitored vendors.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (11)

Each is something that could not be established from public sources. Export them as a list to send, with empty columns for the vendor’s answer and the evidence supplied.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. How many full-time employees does UpGuard have, and how many work in engineering and customer support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a large bank deployment.
  2. Has UpGuard reduced headcount in the last 24 months, and by how much?
    Layoffs can reduce support and product capacity and signal financial pressure.
  3. Has UpGuard had any security incident affecting customer data or service in the last five years, and how was each disclosed and remediated?
    A supplier's own security incidents show how well it protects customer data.
  4. Has UpGuard lost any major financial-services customers in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of major customers can indicate product or service problems.
  5. Have any current or former UpGuard executives or directors been the subject of misconduct findings or disputes with the company?
    Leadership disputes or misconduct can disrupt a supplier and harm its customers' reputation.
  6. How much capital has UpGuard raised in total, and what is its current cash runway?
    Total capital raised helps a bank judge the vendor's financial staying power.
  7. Does UpGuard Vendor Risk produce the DORA register of information or other supervisory third-party registers, and in which formats?
    EU and UK supervisors require registers of third-party arrangements; a bank needs to know whether the product produces them.
  8. How are UpGuard security ratings calculated, and how can a rated supplier dispute a finding?
    A bank needs to understand how a supplier's rating is derived before relying on it.
Show the other 3
  1. Is customer data in Vendor Risk, including questionnaire responses and uploaded documents, used to train or fine-tune any AI model by UpGuard, Google or OpenAI?
    A bank must know whether its supplier assessments and documents are used to train AI models.
  2. Does UpGuard support customer-managed encryption keys?
    Customer-managed keys let a bank revoke a supplier's access to its data.
  3. Has UpGuard had any breach of customer data in the last five years, and what was disclosed to customers?
    A history of breaches of customer data is a direct indicator of data risk.
