Cloud directory platform that combines workforce identity, access and device management. It provides a cloud directory, single sign-on over SAML and OIDC, multi-factor authentication, cloud LDAP and RADIUS, conditional access, access requests and certification, and management of Windows, Apple, Linux and Android devices, sold in per-user packages. It is aimed mainly at IT teams and managed service providers.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (9)

Each is something that could not be established from public sources. Export them as a list to send, with empty columns for the vendor’s answer and the evidence supplied.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. In what year was JumpCloud Inc. founded?
    Company age is an input to maturity and viability.
  2. How many full-time employees does JumpCloud have, and how many work in engineering and customer support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a large bank deployment.
  3. Has JumpCloud reduced headcount in the last 24 months, and by how much?
    Workforce reductions can reduce support and product capacity.
  4. Has JumpCloud lost any major regulated-industry customer in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of major customers can signal product or service problems.
  5. Have any of JumpCloud's executives or directors been the subject of misconduct findings or disputes in the last five years?
    Leadership disputes or misconduct can disrupt a supplier and harm those associated with it.
  6. What does JumpCloud's Agentic IAM feature do for service accounts, workloads and AI agents, and is it generally available?
    Service accounts, workloads and AI agents are a growing share of identities a bank must control.
  7. Does JumpCloud, or any AI sub-processor it uses (Google, OpenAI, Anthropic), use customer data to train or tune models, and can the bank disable AI-enabled features?
    Directory, credential and device data sent to AI features could be used to train models unless the contract prevents it.
  8. Can a customer supply and control the encryption keys used for its JumpCloud tenant data?
    Control of encryption keys limits the vendor's and its hosts' access to bank data.
