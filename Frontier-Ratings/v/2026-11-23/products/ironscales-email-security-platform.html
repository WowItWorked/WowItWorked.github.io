Email security platform that connects to Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace by API at mailbox level. It combines machine-learning detection of phishing, business email compromise and account takeover with automated clustering and remediation (Themis), a report-phishing button, crowdsourced threat intelligence, phishing simulation and awareness training, DMARC management and outbound email encryption. Sold direct and through managed service providers in tiered plans with add-ons.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

  2. Has IRONSCALES reduced headcount in the last 24 months, and by how much?

  3. Is IRONSCALES party to any material litigation?
    Litigation can signal product, contract or conduct problems.
  4. Has IRONSCALES experienced any security incident affecting its corporate or production systems, and how was each disclosed?
    A security vendor's own incidents bear directly on the trust a bank places in it.
  5. Has IRONSCALES lost any enterprise or financial-services customers in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of large customers can signal product or service problems.
  6. Have any of IRONSCALES's executives or directors left in the last 24 months in connection with a dispute, investigation or misconduct finding?
    Leadership instability or misconduct affects vendor reliability.
  7. What message, detection and administrator logs does the platform keep, for how long, and how can they be exported?
    Banks need message and administrative logs to investigate incidents.
  8. Does the platform keep an audit trail of administrator and policy changes, and what is its retention and export?
    An audit trail of administrator actions is needed for control testing.
Show the other 8
  1. What explanation does the platform give for each verdict and automated remediation by Themis?
    Analysts and auditors need to see why a message was classified as malicious.
  2. What reports or evidence packs can IRONSCALES produce for auditors and regulators, and in what formats?
    Banks need reports they can hand to auditors and examiners.
  3. Can IRONSCALES provide its current list of sub-processors with purpose and processing location?
