A hosted platform that overlays an organisation's security tools on a profile of the adversary behaviour relevant to it, using MITRE ATT&CK, to show covered and uncovered techniques, score confidence in the defences and recommend changes. A free Community Edition also exists and is not evaluated here.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (24)

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. On what date, and by what filing, did Tidal Security, Inc. change its legal name to Tidal Cyber, Inc., and is it the same legal entity?
    The company was first called Tidal Security Inc. and a bank needs the exact legal name and its history to check contracts, registers and screening results.
  2. Has Tidal Cyber made any workforce reductions in the last 24 months, and if so how many roles and which teams were affected?
    Workforce reductions can signal financial pressure and can affect support and product development.
  3. Has Tidal Cyber, Inc. been subject to any regulatory investigation, enforcement action, fine or order in any jurisdiction?
    Regulatory action against a supplier is a factor in third-party risk assessment.
  4. Is Tidal Cyber, Inc. a party to any pending or recently resolved litigation, and what is each matter about?
    Material litigation can affect a supplier's finances and its ability to deliver.
  5. Has Tidal Cyber had any security incident or breach affecting its platform, corporate systems or customer data, and how were customers told?
    A supplier's own security incidents bear directly on the risk of giving it data about the bank's defences.
  6. Has Tidal Cyber lost any significant enterprise customer in the last 24 months, and what was the stated reason?
    Loss of significant customers can indicate product or service problems.
  7. Have there been any departures of founders or executives, or any conduct matters involving Tidal Cyber's leadership, in the last 24 months?
    Leadership instability or misconduct can affect a supplier's direction and reliability.
  8. Does Enterprise Edition read the actual configuration, enabled rules or telemetry of our security tools, or is the defensive stack built from the product registry and what we enter by hand?
    A coverage map built from what a tool can do in principle overstates protection if the tool is not configured that way.
