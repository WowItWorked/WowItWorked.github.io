How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Open as a question list
  1. In what year was Sardine founded, and under which legal entity?
    The founding year dates the vendor's track record.
  2. How many full-time employees does Sardine have, and how many work in engineering and customer support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a large bank deployment.
  3. Has Sardine reduced headcount in the last 24 months, and by how much?
    Layoffs can reduce support and product capacity.
  4. Will Sardine provide its latest audited financial statements for due diligence?
    Financial statements let a bank assess the vendor's financial soundness.
  5. Is Sardine party to any material litigation, including intellectual-property or privacy claims?
    Material litigation can threaten a vendor's finances or its right to sell the product.
  6. Has Sardine had any security incident affecting customer data or service in the last five years, and how was each disclosed?
    Past security incidents and how they were handled indicate the vendor's security maturity.
  7. Has Sardine lost any financial-institution customer in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of major customers can signal product or service problems.
  8. Have there been any leadership departures, disputes or conduct findings at Sardine in the last 24 months that a customer should know about?
    Leadership disputes or misconduct can disrupt a vendor.
Show the other 13
  1. What is the pricing model and list price for Sardine's product at a bank's expected volume?
