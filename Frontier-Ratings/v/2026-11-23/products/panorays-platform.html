Third-party cyber risk management SaaS platform. It rates suppliers from an outside-in assessment of their internet-facing assets across network, application and human layers, combines that with questionnaires that AI helps answer and check against uploaded evidence, discovers fourth and further parties, and monitors suppliers for breaches, dark web mentions and security changes. It includes remediation tasks, board and standards reports, a DORA register of information add-on, a Trust Center for publishing a company's own security documents, and a cyber risk quantification module launched in August 2026. Hosted on Google Cloud.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. In what year was Panorays founded, and when did its platform become generally available?
    Company age is an input to maturity.
  2. How many full-time employees does Panorays have, and how many work in engineering and customer support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a large bank deployment.
  3. Has Panorays reduced headcount in the last 24 months, and by how much?
    Layoffs can reduce support and product capacity and signal financial pressure.
  4. Is Panorays party to any material litigation, current or concluded in the last five years?
    Material litigation can affect a supplier's finances and continuity.
  5. Has Panorays had any security incident affecting customer or supplier data in the last five years, and how was each disclosed?
    A third-party risk platform holds suppliers' security evidence, so its own incidents matter to many firms.
  6. Has Panorays lost any large regulated customer in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of large customers can signal product or service problems.
  7. Have there been any departures of founders or senior executives, or any conduct findings against them, in the last 24 months?
    Leadership instability or misconduct can affect delivery and reputation.
  8. What user-activity audit trail does Panorays provide to customers, how long is it kept, and can it be exported?
    A bank needs a record of who assessed, approved or overrode supplier risk decisions.
Show the other 10
  1. Does Panorays provide a supported SIEM integration or log export for alerts and user activity?
    Banks route supplier-risk alerts and audit logs into a SIEM.
  2. Does Panorays use customer inputs, outputs or assessment data to train or tune AI models, are those models shared across customers, and can a customer opt out?
    A bank needs to know whether its assessment data or its suppliers' answers train AI models shared with other customers.
  3. Does Panorays support customer-managed encryption keys?
    Customer-managed keys let a bank revoke a supplier's access to its data.
  4. Has Panorays had any breach of customer or supplier data, and can the incident reports be provided?
    A history of breaches of customer or supplier data bears directly on data risk.
  5. Does Panorays run a status page or incident log, and can it share its availability record for the last 24 months?
    Outage history shows how reliable the service has been.
  6. What support tiers, hours, response targets and availability SLA does Panorays offer, and from which locations is support delivered?
    A bank needs defined support hours, response targets and an availability commitment.
  7. From which countries do Panorays support and engineering staff access customer data?
    Where support staff sit determines who can access a bank's data.
  8. What audit trail, log export, API and SIEM integration does Panorays provide to customers?
    A bank needs audit records and exports from the tool it uses to make supplier decisions.
  9. Can Panorays share its most recent funding round, current cash runway and audited or summary financial statements?
    A bank needs to know the supplier will be supporting the product in three years.
