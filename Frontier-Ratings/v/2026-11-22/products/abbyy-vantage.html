Intelligent document processing platform built around trainable 'skills' for classification and extraction, with a catalogue of pre-trained skills, a low-code Skill Designer, manual review, and optional prompt-based extraction through customer or ABBYY-supplied LLM connections. Offered as ABBYY-hosted cloud in the EU, US or Australia, or self-hosted on Azure Kubernetes Service or customer-run Kubernetes. Version 3.0 became generally available in February 2026.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.
