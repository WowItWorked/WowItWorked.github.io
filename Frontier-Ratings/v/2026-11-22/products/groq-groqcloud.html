Groq's inference cloud, which the vendor now describes as a neocloud for fast inference. It serves open-weight models through an API on Groq's LPU processors, with on-demand, flex, performance and batch service tiers, and the platform page describes dedicated bare-metal infrastructure (GroqMetal), an inference stack (GroqCore) and a governance layer (GroqAssured). After licensing its chip technology to NVIDIA in December 2025, Groq says it also deploys clusters of NVIDIA accelerated computing as an NVIDIA Cloud Partner.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Artificial Analysis API provider benchmarks · Rolling five-day window, read 2026-11-22

Artificial Analysis · published 2026-11-22

Artificial Analysis benchmarks six open-weight model endpoints served by Groq. Time to first answer token ranged from 1.22 seconds (gpt-oss-120b, low reasoning) to 7.52 seconds, blended prices from US$0.05 to US$0.20 per million tokens, and error rates from 0.0% to 6.7%. On gpt-oss-120b at high reasoning Groq took 3.5 minutes per benchmark task and 5.68 seconds to first answer token, against 1.0 minute and 2.63 seconds on Cerebras, at about a third of Cerebras's price.
