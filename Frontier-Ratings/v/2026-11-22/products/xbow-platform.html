Hosted platform that runs AI agents against running web applications and APIs, confirms each finding with a working exploit before reporting it, and retests after a fix. A coordinator directs many short-lived agents; separate validators and a safety model check their work. The vendor states it does not test networks, mobile apps, physical sites or people.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Open as a question list
  1. Has XBOW made any workforce reductions in the last 24 months, and if so when and how large?
    Workforce cuts can signal a funding or strategy problem.
  2. Has XBOW been the subject of any regulatory investigation, enforcement action or order in any jurisdiction?

  3. Is XBOW party to any pending or recently resolved litigation, and what is each matter about?
    Material litigation can affect a supplier's finances and attention.
  4. Has XBOW had any security incident affecting its platform or customer data, and how was each one disclosed?
    An offensive-testing supplier holds credentials and exploit evidence for the bank's applications.
  5. Has XBOW lost any named reference customer in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of reference customers can indicate product or service problems.
  6. Have any XBOW executives or founders left or been the subject of a dispute in the last 24 months?
    Leadership disputes or sudden departures can affect delivery.
  7. What was XBOW's revenue in its last full financial year, and is it audited?
    Revenue shows whether the supplier is sustained by customers or by investors.
  8. Which analyst reports place XBOW, and in what position?
    Analyst placements are one public signal of market standing.

  1. Can XBOW provide its current sub-processor list with the purpose and processing location of each?

  3. Has any XBOW customer data been exposed in a security incident, and what was disclosed?
