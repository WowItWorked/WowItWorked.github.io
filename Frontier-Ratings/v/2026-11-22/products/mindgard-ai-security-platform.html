Platform the vendor describes as offensive security for AI systems and agents. Its modules are Recon (maps the models, agents, tools, permissions and guardrails of an AI system), Attack and AI Red Teaming (automated one-shot and multi-step attacks, run on demand or in CI/CD), Remediate (turns findings into fixes), Assure (continuous checks against AI policy) and AI Threat Response (run-time detection and response). Expert-led red-teaming services are sold alongside. The start date in the ownership history is the vendor's founding year by convention; the product's launch date was not found.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (21)

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. How many people does Mindgard employ, and how many work in engineering and customer support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a bank customer.
  2. Has Mindgard made any workforce reductions since it was founded?
    Layoffs can signal financial stress or reduced support capacity.
  3. Can Mindgard provide its latest filed accounts and current cash position?
    Filed accounts show cash and losses for a private supplier.
  4. Has Mindgard been subject to any regulatory investigation, order or penalty?
    Regulatory action against a supplier bears on a bank's own supervisory standing.
  5. Is Mindgard party to any material litigation?
    Material litigation can affect a private supplier's finances.
  6. Has Mindgard had any security incident affecting its systems or customer data?
    A security vendor's own incidents bear directly on the trust a bank can place in it.
  7. Has Mindgard lost any large customer in the last two years, and why?
    Loss of large customers can signal product or service problems.
  8. Have there been any departures of founders or senior executives, or governance disputes, at Mindgard?
    Leadership turnover or disputes can disrupt a young supplier.
Show the other 13
  1. What is Mindgard's pricing metric and list price for the platform and for red-teaming services?
    Pricing metric and list price are needed to compare cost.
  2. What would a deployment covering our AI applications cost per year?
    An estimate helps budget where list prices are not public.
  3. Does Mindgard scan model files or AI dependencies for malicious code and tampering?
    Model files and AI components from public sources can carry malicious code.
  4. Does Mindgard's run-time module detect and stop sensitive data in prompts and responses, or does the platform only test whether an application can be made to leak data?
    Testing for leakage is different from stopping sensitive data in live prompts and responses.
  5. What activity logs does the Mindgard platform keep of tests run, targets and users, and can customers view them?
    A bank needs a record of which tests were run against which systems and by whom.
  6. Does the platform keep an audit trail of user and administrator actions, and how long is it retained?
    Administrator and test activity should be auditable.
  7. Is customer data, including prompts, responses and results from tests of our systems, used to train or tune Mindgard's models, and are those models shared across customers?
    Test results and customer system details could be used to improve attack models shared across customers.
  8. Does Mindgard support customer-managed encryption keys?
    Customer-held keys limit the vendor's access to stored data.
  9. Has the Mindgard platform had any security incident affecting customer data?
    Past breaches of a service that holds details of weaknesses in a bank's AI systems are especially sensitive.
  10. What service incidents has the platform had, and is there a status page?
    Service incidents show how reliably tests in a release pipeline can run.
  11. Which model providers does Mindgard use to generate attacks and evaluate responses, and is customer content sent to them?
    Models used to generate attacks or judge responses may be third-party services that see customer content.
