A Chromium-based browser for work that applies access, data and device-posture policy inside the browser itself: zero-trust access to private applications without a VPN, controls on copy, paste, download, print and screenshot, web threat protection, and detailed activity logging. It is the core of the Island Enterprise Platform, which extends the same policies to other browsers through an extension, to desktop applications and to network services.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (19)

Each is something that could not be established from public sources. Export them as a list to send, with empty columns for the vendor’s answer and the evidence supplied.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. Has Island made any workforce reductions in the last three years?
    Layoffs can signal financial stress or reduced support capacity.
  2. Has Island been the subject of any regulatory investigation or enforcement action?
    Enforcement history bears on a supplier's conduct risk.
  3. Is Island party to any material litigation, pending or resolved in the last three years?
    Material litigation can affect a supplier's finances and continuity.
  4. Has Island had any security incident affecting its management service or its browser's update channel, and what vulnerabilities has it disclosed?
    A browser that carries all of a bank's web sessions makes the supplier's own security record critical.
  5. Have any founders or senior executives left Island in the last two years, or been subject to adverse findings?
    Leadership instability affects delivery and support.
  6. What is Island's pricing basis and list price per user, for the browser and for the network and AI services?
    Without a pricing basis a bank cannot estimate cost at enterprise scale.
  7. In which regions can an Island tenant and its logs be hosted, and can the customer choose?
    A bank may need session logs and policy processing kept in a chosen jurisdiction.
  8. What reason does Island record when a policy blocks or allows an action, including for its AI governance functions?
    Where a policy or AI function blocks an action, the bank must be able to see why.
Show the other 11
  1. Can Island provide its current sub-processor list with purposes and locations?
    Sub-processors are fourth parties that may handle the bank's data.
  2. Is any customer data, including prompts handled by Island's AI services, used to train models, and are those models shared across customers?
    The platform includes AI services; the bank must know whether its browsing data or prompts train models.
  3. How does Island encrypt stored activity logs and session data at rest and in transit?
    Encryption of stored logs and recordings is a baseline control.
  4. Can a customer supply and control the encryption keys for its Island tenant, or store logs in its own storage?
    Customer-held keys limit the supplier's access to stored data.
  5. Has Island had any breach of customer data?
    Past breaches indicate how well the supplier protects what it holds.
  6. Does Island publish service status and incident history, and what incidents occurred in the last 24 months?
    Outage history shows how reliable the management service is.
  7. Which AI model providers sit behind Island's AI assistant and AI services, and where do they run?
    Third-party model providers are fourth parties that may process the bank's data.
  8. What support tiers, hours and response targets does Island offer?
    Support hours and response targets determine how quickly a fault affecting every user's browser is resolved.
  9. On termination, what policy and log data can a customer export, and when is stored data deleted?
    A bank needs to know how policies and logs are exported and how users move back to another browser.
  10. From which countries do Island support staff access customer tenants?
    Support staff location determines who can access the bank's data.
  11. Where is tenant data hosted, can the customer choose the region, and from which countries is support delivered?
    Hosting regions, sub-processors and support locations could not be read, so country exposure cannot be placed on the scale.
