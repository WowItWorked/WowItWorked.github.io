Cloud-native SIEM with built-in automation, which Microsoft's documentation now calls Microsoft Sentinel SIEM and positions as part of a wider Sentinel security platform with a data lake, graph and MCP server. It collects data through connectors, detects with analytics rules and behaviour analytics, and supports hunting, incident investigation and playbooks built on Azure Logic Apps. It is operated from the Microsoft Defender portal; Microsoft states that the Azure portal experience will not be supported after 31 March 2027. The ownership start date is the vendor's founding year by dataset convention, as no launch date was sourced.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (8)

Each is something that could not be established from public sources. Export them as a list to send, with empty columns for the vendor’s answer and the evidence supplied.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. Who are the largest shareholders of Microsoft Corporation and does any hold a controlling interest?
    Large shareholders can influence a critical provider's strategy.
  2. Have any financial-services customers ended or materially reduced their use of Microsoft Entra, Microsoft Sentinel, Microsoft Foundry, Copilot Studio or Microsoft 365 Copilot in the last 24 months, and why?
    Departures of large or regulated customers can signal product or service problems.
  3. Have there been any executive departures, governance findings or conduct issues at Microsoft in the last 24 months that affect the security, identity or AI businesses?
    Leadership instability or misconduct at a critical provider is a reputational and continuity signal.
  4. Which sub-processors on the Microsoft Online Services Subprocessors List process customer data for this service, where are they located, and how much notice does Microsoft give before adding one?
    Sub-processors are fourth parties with access to the bank's data; the bank must be able to list and assess them.
  5. Is data we ingest into Microsoft Sentinel used to train or tune detection, UEBA or AI models that serve other customers, and can we opt out?
    Security telemetry is sensitive; a bank needs to know whether it trains detection or AI models used for other customers.
  6. Has any security incident in the last five years exposed customer data held in Microsoft Sentinel or Log Analytics workspaces?
    A history of customer-data exposure in the SIEM would bear directly on its use as the system of record.
  7. Which AI model providers process our security data when Security Copilot or Sentinel MCP tools are used with Microsoft Sentinel?
    AI-assisted triage depends on model providers that become fourth parties.
  8. From which countries do Microsoft support and engineering staff access customer data for this service, and can that access be limited to chosen jurisdictions?
    A bank needs to know from which countries support staff can reach its tenant or data.
