Data security platform built on data lineage: it traces where data originated and how people and AI agents copy and move it, and uses that context for data loss prevention, insider risk management, data security posture management and controls on AI tools. It uses an endpoint agent, a browser extension and cloud connectors, with a hosted back end. Flow is the name the vendor gave the platform in July 2026.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

  1. Has Cyberhaven reduced its workforce in the last 24 months, and if so when and by how much?

  2. Has Cyberhaven been subject to any regulatory investigation, order or penalty in the last five years, including any arising from the December 2024 browser extension incident, and what was the outcome?
    Regulatory action against a data security supplier bears on a bank's own supervisory standing.
  3. Is Cyberhaven party to any pending or recently resolved material litigation, including any claims arising from the December 2024 browser extension incident?
    Material litigation can affect a vendor's finances and focus.
  4. Have any financial-services customers ended their use of Cyberhaven in the last 24 months, and why?
    Departures of large customers can signal product or trust problems.
  5. Have there been any disputes, investigations or unplanned departures involving Cyberhaven's senior leadership in the last 24 months beyond the May 2025 chief executive change?
    Leadership conduct or instability can affect delivery and trust.
  6. Is customer data used to train or tune Linea AI or any other model, and if so are those models shared across customers?
    A bank needs to know whether its data is used to train models shared with other customers.
  7. Can a customer supply and control its own encryption keys for data held in its Cyberhaven instance?
    Customer-held keys limit the vendor's and host's ability to read stored data.
  8. Does Cyberhaven publish a service status page, and what availability incidents has the service had in the last 24 months?
    Outage history shows how reliable the service has been.
