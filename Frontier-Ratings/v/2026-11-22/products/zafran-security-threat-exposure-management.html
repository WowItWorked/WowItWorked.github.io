Threat exposure management platform that takes vulnerability findings from scanners, cloud and application security tools and its own detection, removes duplicates, and ranks them by whether the weakness is running, reachable, being exploited and already covered by the customer's existing security controls. It proposes mitigations using those controls, groups findings that share a fix, and routes work to owners through ticketing tools. AI agents investigate exposure to newly disclosed flaws and draft remediation, with approvals.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Timeline

  1. 2026-11-18 · launch Zafran Security
    Zafran announces Offensive Validation, AI-agent attack testing for its exposure platform

    Zafran announced Offensive Validation on its website on 2026-11-18 as a new part of its exposure management platform. Zafran says AI agents test internal and internet-facing assets to show which exposures are actually exploitable, check whether mitigations and fixes hold, and run inside isolated sandboxes under customer-set rules of engagement. The post does not state whether the feature is generally available or give pricing. Zafran Security (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-22, opens in new tab)

  2. 2026-08-03 · launch Zafran Security
    Zafran launches Attack Chain Killswitch with Google Threat Intelligence

    Zafran announced Attack Chain Killswitch, which draws multi-step attack paths across cloud and on-premises systems using Google Threat Intelligence and Mandiant investigations, scores each chain and points to the mitigation or fix that breaks the most chains. Choosing a fix creates a work item routed to the owner in a ticketing tool. Availability was not stated. Zafran Security (vendor, retrieved 2026-11-21, opens in new tab)

  3. 2025-12-02 · funding Zafran Security
    Zafran Security raises US$60 million Series C led by Menlo Ventures

    SecurityWeek and SiliconANGLE reported that Zafran raised a US$60 million Series C led by Menlo Ventures, with Sequoia Capital, Cyberstarts, PSP Growth, Vintage Investment Partners and Knollwood Investment, bringing total funding to US$130 million. Alongside the round the company introduced Agentic Exposure Management, which uses AI agents to check exposure to new flaws, propose mitigations and find the owner of a vulnerable system.
