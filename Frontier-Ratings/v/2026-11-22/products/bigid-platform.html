Data security, privacy and governance platform built on discovery and classification of structured, unstructured and semi-structured data across cloud, SaaS, on-premises and AI environments. It adds posture management, access governance, remediation workflows and AI data governance, and feeds classification context to third-party data loss prevention tools. The vendor's site calls it the BigID Data Security Platform; the current AWS Marketplace listing is named BigID Next.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (14)

Each is something that could not be established from public sources. Export them as a list to send, with empty columns for the vendor’s answer and the evidence supplied.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. Has BigID had any security incident or unauthorised access affecting its corporate systems, website or customer environments in the last three years, including in 2026, and how were customers notified?
    A security vendor's own incident record indicates how well it protects the access it is given to bank data.
  2. Which large customers have not renewed in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of major customers can signal product or service problems.
  3. Have any founders or executives left BigID in the last 24 months, and are there any governance disputes?
    Leadership disputes or departures can affect delivery and governance.
  4. What logs of scans, findings, remediation actions and administrator activity does BigID keep, for how long, and how can a customer access them?
    Logs of product activity are needed for the bank's own monitoring and investigations.
  5. How does BigID explain an individual classification result or risk finding, and can the explanation be exported?
    A bank needs to understand why data was classified or a risk raised in order to act on it and defend the decision.
  6. What APIs, webhooks or file exports does BigID provide for findings, activity and audit data?
    Export of findings and logs lets a bank feed its own monitoring and reporting.
  7. Which SIEM and SOAR products does BigID integrate with, and what events does it send?
    SIEM integration lets a bank correlate data security events with other telemetry.
  8. Does BigID use customer data, metadata or classification feedback to train or tune models, are any such models shared across customers, and what does Anthropic receive as a sub-processor?
    Use of bank data to train models shared across customers can leak information and breach confidentiality duties.
Show the other 6
  1. Has customer data held or accessed by BigID or its sub-processors ever been exposed without authorisation, including any event in 2026?
    A past breach of customer data is directly relevant to the risk of giving the vendor access to bank data stores.
  2. Does BigID run a status page for its SaaS service, and what incidents affected it in the last 24 months?
    Outage history shows how reliable the service is and how openly the vendor reports problems.
  3. What data export formats, deletion commitments and exit assistance does BigID provide at termination, under its licence agreement and data processing addendum?
    A bank must be able to leave with its data and without disruption.
  4. From which countries do BigID support and engineering staff access customer environments, and can access be restricted to chosen jurisdictions?
    Where support staff sit determines which jurisdictions can reach bank data during troubleshooting.
  5. Can BigID document its audit trail, log export options, SIEM integrations and retention settings?
    The observability score depends on logs, audit trail, export and SIEM integration, none of which could be established.
  6. Who are BigID's shareholders above 10%, in which regions can SaaS data be hosted at the customer's choice, and where are support staff located?
    Country risk depends on ownership, processing locations and where support is delivered.
