Atlassian's AI offering for its cloud products: Rovo Search (enterprise search across Atlassian and connected third-party sources), Rovo Chat (a conversational assistant grounded in that content), Rovo Agents (prebuilt or custom agents that carry out tasks) and Rovo Studio for building agents. It is built on Atlassian's Teamwork Graph and is included in paid Jira, Confluence and other cloud plans. Generally available since 2024-10-09. Atlassian's separate coding product, Rovo Dev, is not covered by this record.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

  1. Has Atlassian been subject to any regulatory investigation, order or penalty in the last five years, and what was the outcome?
    Regulatory action against a collaboration and AI supplier bears on a bank's own supervisory standing.
  2. Has Atlassian lost any large financial-services customers of its cloud products in the last two years, and why?
    Loss of large customers can signal product or service problems.
  3. What changes have there been to Atlassian's executive team in the last two years, and were any connected to a dispute or investigation?
    Leadership instability affects delivery of the roadmap a bank depends on.
  4. Is de-identified metadata from our organisation used to fine-tune Atlassian-hosted models shared across customers, is that on by default, and how do we switch it off?
    A bank needs to know whether its content or metadata improves models used by other customers.
  5. Does Rovo's index of our content, including content ingested from third-party apps, support customer-managed encryption keys?
    Customer control of encryption keys limits the vendor's and its hosts' access to bank data.
  6. Has any incident exposed Rovo-indexed content or chat data to people not entitled to see it, and what was done?
    Past exposure of indexed content shows how permission controls hold up in practice.
