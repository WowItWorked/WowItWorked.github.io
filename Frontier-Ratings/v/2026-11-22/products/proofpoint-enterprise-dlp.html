How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (12)

Each is something that could not be established from public sources. Export them as a list to send, with empty columns for the vendor’s answer and the evidence supplied.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. Has Proofpoint reduced its workforce in the last 24 months, and by how much in the teams supporting the products we use?
    Workforce cuts at a privately held, leveraged supplier are an early signal of pressure on support and development.
  2. Have any large financial-services customers left Proofpoint in the last 24 months, and why?
    Departures of large or regulated customers can indicate product or service problems.
  3. Have there been any unplanned executive departures, or findings concerning executive conduct, at Proofpoint in the last 24 months?
    Leadership instability or misconduct at a supplier is a reputational and continuity risk.
  4. Is any customer data processed by Enterprise DLP used to train or tune models that are shared across customers, and can a customer opt out?
    A bank needs to know whether its content is used to train models that serve other customers.
  5. Does Enterprise DLP support customer-managed or customer-held encryption keys for data at rest, and for which components?
    Control of encryption keys limits the supplier's and its hosts' access to bank data.
  6. Which third-party AI model providers, if any, process customer data for Enterprise DLP, and under what terms?
    Third-party model providers are fourth parties that may receive bank data.
  7. What service incidents has Enterprise DLP had in the last 24 months, with duration and root cause, and where is that history published?
    Outage history shows how reliable the service has been and how openly the supplier reports failures.
  8. What explanation does Enterprise DLP give for each detection or policy decision, and can that explanation be exported?
    Analysts and auditors need to see why a message or data movement was judged risky.
Show the other 4
  1. What reports or evidence packs does Enterprise DLP produce for auditors and regulators, and against which frameworks?
    Banks must show auditors and supervisors that controls operated.
  2. Does Enterprise DLP tokenise or mask sensitive values, or only detect, block and redact them?
    Masking or tokenising sensitive values is one way to keep data usable while reducing exposure.
  3. How can Enterprise DLP alerts, incidents and user-activity records be exported (API, streaming, file), in what format and with what limits?
    A bank's security operations team needs DLP events in its own tooling.
  4. Can Proofpoint provide the Enterprise DLP documentation for administrator audit logs, log export and SIEM integration?
    The observability score needs documented audit trails and export methods for this product, which were not found.
