Hosted document processing and analytics platform for lenders. It classifies and captures data from bank statements, pay stubs, tax forms and more than 2,100 other form types, with a fully automated mode and a mode that adds human verification by the vendor's own staff, and layers fraud detection, cash-flow analytics and mortgage income calculation on top. Accessed by API, dashboard and widget, with integrations to loan origination systems.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Open as a question list
  1. How many full-time employees does Ocrolus have, and how many work in engineering and customer support?

  2. Has Ocrolus reduced headcount in the last 24 months, and by how much?
    Layoffs can reduce support and product capacity.
  3. Has Ocrolus had any security incident affecting customer data or production systems in the last five years, and how was each disclosed?
    Past incidents and how they were handled show the maturity of the vendor's security programme.
  4. Has Ocrolus lost any financial-services customer with annual contract value above US$1 million in the last 24 months?
    Loss of major customers can signal product or service problems.
  5. Have there been any departures of the chief executive, finance, technology or security leaders in the last 24 months, or any conduct findings against executives of Ocrolus?
    Leadership instability or misconduct affects delivery and reputation.
  6. Can a customer train or fine-tune models in Ocrolus on its own documents, and are those models isolated to that customer?
    Banks often need models for their own document types.
  7. What system, access and processing logs does Ocrolus make available to customers, and how long are they kept?
    A bank needs system and access logs to investigate errors and misuse.
  8. Which SIEM products does Ocrolus integrate with out of the box, and by what mechanism are audit and system logs streamed?
    A bank's security operations centre needs product logs in its SIEM.
Show the other 3
  1. Is customer document data or data derived from it used to train or tune models that serve other customers of Ocrolus, and can a customer opt out by contract?

  2. Does Ocrolus support customer-managed encryption keys, and in which deployment models?
    Customer-managed keys let a bank revoke the vendor's access to its data.
  3. Has there ever been unauthorised access to customer data processed by Ocrolus, and if so when and how was it remediated?
    A prior breach of customer data is a direct indicator of data risk.
