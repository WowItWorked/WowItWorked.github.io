Document processing API and no-code Studio. Parse turns PDFs, scans, spreadsheets and slides into structured JSON; Extract returns typed fields with citations and bounding boxes; Split, Classify and Edit segment, route and fill documents. It combines the vendor's own models with third-party foundation models and is sold as a hosted API, with VPC and on-premises deployment for enterprise customers.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

  2. Has Reducto made any workforce reductions in the last 24 months, and what is its current headcount trend?

  3. Has Reducto been subject to any regulatory investigation, enforcement action or order in the last five years?
    Regulatory action against a vendor can disrupt service and signals control weaknesses.
  4. Is Reducto a party to any material litigation or arbitration, pending or resolved in the last five years?
    Material litigation can affect the vendor's finances and its right to supply the product.
  5. What security incidents has Reducto had in the last five years, and what was the impact on customers?
    Past incidents show how the vendor protects systems and how it responds.
  6. Has Reducto lost any major customers in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of large customers can signal product or service problems.
  7. Have there been any departures, disputes or conduct matters involving the senior leadership of Reducto in the last 24 months?
    Leadership instability or misconduct can affect delivery and governance.
  8. Does Reducto keep an audit trail of user and administrator actions, and what does each record contain?
    Audit trails are needed to reconstruct who did what and when.

  1. Can logs and usage telemetry from Reducto be exported by API or stream, and in what format?
