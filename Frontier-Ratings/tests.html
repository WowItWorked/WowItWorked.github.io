What happened when someone other than the vendor ran the test. 63 of 314 tracked products appear in at least one of the 67 public test programmes checked.

How to read this

  • A test is listed only if it was run by a party other than the vendor and its results are published where anyone can read them.
  • Each programme states who pays. A vendor-commissioned test is weaker evidence than one the tester funds, and is labelled.
  • Results are summarised in our own words with the tester’s published figures. A result is a snapshot of one version on one date.
  • Not appearing in a test says nothing about a product. Many vendors do not take part, and whole categories have no public test.
  • Test results do not change a score by themselves. Where a score relies on one, its reasoning says so.
