How it earns your trust

Three rules we do not bend

Every fact is sourced

Each data point links to the page it came from, with its retrieval date and whether it is the vendor’s own claim or independently evidenced.

“No public evidence” is a real answer

If something could not be found in public sources, it is shown as “no public evidence” and turned into a question to ask the vendor. It never counts as zero. 4,868 are open now.

The method is published

The rubric, weights, tiers and validation rules are on the methodology page. No vendor pays to be listed. Research and scoring are done by an AI agent, without human review before publication.
