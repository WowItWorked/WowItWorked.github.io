Salesforce's platform for building and running AI agents on its CRM, data and workflow services. Salesforce calls it the agentic layer of the Agentforce 360 Platform. It includes Agentforce Builder and the Agent Script language, the Atlas Reasoning Engine, a testing centre and API, observability with session tracing, a Model Context Protocol client and registry, and the Einstein Trust Layer for masking, toxicity checks and audit data. It was formerly called Einstein Copilot.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Open as a question list
  1. Which shareholders hold 5% or more of Salesforce's voting stock, and in which countries are they based?
    Large or controlling shareholders affect ownership and country risk.
  2. Have any financial-services customers ended or materially reduced their use of Agentforce in the last two years, and why?

  3. Does Salesforce use Agentforce customer data, including aggregated or de-identified data, to train or tune any Salesforce-owned or shared model, and can a customer opt out?
    A bank needs to know whether its data is used to train models, including the vendor's own models.
