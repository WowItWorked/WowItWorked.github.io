Securiti's platform for data and AI security, governance and privacy, sold under the Securiti name and, since May 2026, as the basis of the Veeam DataAI Command Platform. Its security products cover data discovery and classification, posture management, access intelligence and governance, data flow governance, breach impact analysis, firewalls for LLM prompts and retrieval, and Agent Commander for AI agents. Veeam's October 2025 announcement called the product the Data Command Center.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (15)

Each is something that could not be established from public sources. Export them as a list to send, with empty columns for the vendor’s answer and the evidence supplied.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. Has headcount in the Securiti business been reduced since the Veeam acquisition closed, and by how much?
    Post-acquisition staff cuts can reduce support and product capacity.
  2. Is Securiti party to any material litigation?
    Material litigation can affect a vendor's finances and its freedom to sell the product.
  3. Has Securiti had any security incident affecting its corporate systems or platform, and how were customers notified?
    A security vendor's own incident record indicates how well it protects the access it is given to bank data.
  4. Which large customers have not renewed since the Veeam acquisition, and why?
    Loss of major customers after an acquisition can signal product or service problems.
  5. Which Securiti executives have left since the Veeam acquisition closed, and who now leads product and engineering?
    Leadership departures after an acquisition can affect delivery.
  6. What is the pricing metric and list price for Securiti's data security products, and how is it packaged with Veeam products?
    Pricing structure affects total cost and the terms of exit.
  7. What logs of scans, findings, policy actions and administrator activity does the platform keep, for how long, and how can a customer access them?
    Logs of product activity are needed for the bank's own monitoring and investigations.
  8. How does the platform explain an individual classification result or risk finding, and can the explanation be exported?
    A bank needs to understand why data was classified or a risk raised in order to act on it and defend the decision.
Show the other 7
  1. Which SIEM and SOAR products does the platform integrate with, and what events does it send?
    SIEM integration lets a bank correlate data security events with other telemetry.
  2. The customer agreement licenses Securiti to use customer data for enhancing its products or services: does that include training or tuning models, are any such models shared across customers, and what do Anthropic and Microsoft Azure receive?
    Use of bank data to train models shared across customers can leak information and breach confidentiality duties.
  3. Can a customer supply and control its own encryption keys for data held in the Securiti cloud?
    Control of encryption keys limits vendor and cloud-provider access to bank data.
  4. Has customer data held or accessed by Securiti or its sub-processors ever been exposed without authorisation?
    A past breach of customer data is directly relevant to the risk of giving the vendor access to bank data stores.
  5. From which countries do Securiti and Veeam support and engineering staff access customer tenants, and can access be restricted to chosen jurisdictions?
    Where support staff sit determines which jurisdictions can reach bank data during troubleshooting.
  6. Can Securiti document the platform's audit trail, log export options, SIEM integrations and retention settings?
    The observability score depends on logs, audit trail, export and SIEM integration, which could not be established.
  7. Who owns Veeam above 10%, how does a customer choose its processing region, and where are support and engineering staff with tenant access located?
    Country risk depends on ownership, processing locations and where support is delivered.
