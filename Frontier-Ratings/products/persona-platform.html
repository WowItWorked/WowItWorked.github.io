Identity platform for collecting, verifying and managing information about people and businesses. Its components are Inquiries (data collection flows), Verifications (government ID, selfie, document, database and phone checks), Reports (watchlist and other external data), Workflows (automation), Cases (manual review), Accounts and Graph (link analysis). Integrated as a hosted flow, embedded web flow, mobile SDK or API. A separately provisioned Persona for Government environment is also offered.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Open as a question list
  1. How many full-time employees does Persona have, and how many work in engineering, fraud operations and customer support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a large bank deployment.
  2. How much equity capital has Persona raised in total, and what share of the most recent round was primary capital?
    Total capital raised is an input to judging financial resilience.
  3. Has Persona reduced headcount in the last 24 months, by how much, and in which functions?
    Headcount reductions can reduce support and product capacity.
  4. Have there been any unplanned executive departures, or allegations concerning current executives or directors of Persona, in the last 24 months?
    Leadership instability or misconduct allegations affect vendor reliability.
  5. Does the Persona platform provide deepfake and injection-attack detection, and can Persona share documentation or test results for it?
    The capability is part of the category's core function and a bank needs to know whether it is covered.
  6. What reason codes or explanations does the Persona platform return for each verification decision, and how are automated decisions documented?
    A bank must be able to explain why a customer was accepted or rejected.
  7. What reports or evidence packs can the Persona platform produce for auditors and regulators, and can the vendor share a sample?
    Auditors and supervisors ask for evidence of how identity decisions were made.
  8. Can Persona provide the current list of sub-processors for the Persona platform, with the purpose and processing location of each, and its notice period for changes?
    A bank must know every party that handles its customers' identity data.
Show the other 8
  1. Is data submitted through the Persona platform used to train or improve models that serve other customers, on what legal basis, and can a customer opt out by contract?
    Use of customers' identity documents and biometrics to train shared models is a data-protection and consent question for the bank.
  2. Does the Persona platform support customer-managed encryption keys, and for which data stores?
    Customer-managed keys let a bank revoke access to its data.
  3. Can Persona provide its incident and uptime history for the Persona platform for the last 24 months, with post-incident reviews?
    Outage history indicates how often onboarding could be interrupted.
  4. Which cloud providers and regions host the Persona platform, and is any alternative provider available?
    Cloud dependencies drive concentration risk.
  5. Does the Persona platform rely on any third-party AI or biometric model providers, and which ones?
    Reliance on third-party AI models is a fourth-party dependency.
  6. In which countries is data submitted to the Persona platform processed, including by human reviewers and sub-processors?
    Processing locations determine which transfer rules apply.
  7. From which countries do Persona support and review staff access customer data, and can access be restricted to chosen jurisdictions?
    Support staff with access to identity data may sit in other jurisdictions.
  8. Where is data in the Persona platform stored and processed, from where do support staff access it, and can a customer choose the region?
    Country risk cannot be scored without processing and support locations.
