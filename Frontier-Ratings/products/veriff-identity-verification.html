Identity and document verification service that checks government-issued IDs and matches them to a selfie with liveness detection, offered as a fully automated mode or a hybrid mode with human verification specialists. Options include PEP, sanctions and adverse media screening, registry and database checks, proof of address and biometric authentication. Integrated through mobile and web SDKs and an API, with results delivered by webhook.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Open as a question list
  1. How many full-time employees does Veriff have, and how many work in engineering, fraud operations and customer support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a large bank deployment.
  2. Has Veriff had any security incident affecting customer or end-user data in the last five years, and how was each disclosed and remediated?
    A security incident at an identity verification provider can expose identity documents and biometric data.
  3. Has Veriff lost any customer accounting for a material share of revenue in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of major customers can signal product or trust problems.
  4. Have there been any unplanned executive departures, or allegations concerning current executives or directors of Veriff, in the last 24 months?
    Leadership instability or misconduct allegations affect vendor reliability.
  5. Does Veriff Identity Verification provide reusable and government digital identities, and can Veriff share documentation or test results for it?
    The capability is part of the category's core function and a bank needs to know whether it is covered.
  6. What logs does Veriff Identity Verification make available to the customer, how long are they retained, and how can they be retrieved?
    A bank needs logs of what the service did with each verification to investigate disputes and incidents.
  7. Does Veriff Identity Verification keep an audit trail of user actions and configuration changes, how long is it retained, and can it be exported?
    An audit trail of configuration changes and reviewer actions is needed for internal audit and model governance.
  8. Which SIEM products does Veriff Identity Verification integrate with, and by what mechanism are logs delivered?
    Security teams need vendor activity logs in their SIEM.
Show the other 7
  1. Is data submitted through Veriff Identity Verification used to train or improve models that serve other customers, on what legal basis, and can a customer opt out by contract?
    Use of customers' identity documents and biometrics to train shared models is a data-protection and consent question for the bank.
  2. Does Veriff Identity Verification support customer-managed encryption keys, and for which data stores?
    Customer-managed keys let a bank revoke access to its data.
  3. Has Veriff had any breach of customer or end-user personal data, and can it provide the notifications it issued?
    A history of data breaches is a direct input to data risk.
  4. Does Veriff Identity Verification rely on any third-party AI or biometric model providers, and which ones?
    Reliance on third-party AI models is a fourth-party dependency.
  5. From which countries do Veriff support and review staff access customer data, and can access be restricted to chosen jurisdictions?
    Support staff with access to identity data may sit in other jurisdictions.
  6. What logs, audit trail and export mechanisms does Veriff Identity Verification give a customer, and does it integrate with any SIEM?
    Observability cannot be scored without evidence of logs, audit trail and export.
  7. Where is data in Veriff Identity Verification stored and processed, from where do support staff access it, and can a customer choose the region?
