A cloud email security product, generally available since July 2026, that connects to Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace by API. AI agents assess each message using its content, sender behaviour and the attacker infrastructure mapped in Doppel's threat graph, explain verdicts in plain language, remove or quarantine messages, and request takedown of the sending infrastructure and links. Phishing Triage, for employee-reported messages, and the vendor's training products are sold beside it.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

  4. Has Doppel had any security incident or breach affecting its systems or customer data in the last three years, and how was each one notified and remediated?
    A supplier's own security incidents show how well it protects the customer data it holds.
  5. Has Doppel lost any large enterprise or financial services customer in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of large customers can indicate product or service problems.
  6. Have there been any departures of founders or senior executives, or any governance concerns, at Doppel in the last 24 months?
    Leadership instability or misconduct can disrupt a supplier's delivery and direction.
  7. Does Doppel Email Security detect compromised internal mailboxes, and on what signals?
    Compromised internal mailboxes are used to send fraud from inside the bank.
  8. Does Doppel Email Security manage or report on SPF, DKIM and DMARC for the customer's own domains?
    Sender authentication stops others spoofing the bank's own domains.
