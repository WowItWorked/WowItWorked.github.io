How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Open as a question list
  1. Has Jazz made any workforce reductions since it was founded?
    Layoffs can signal financial stress or reduced support capacity.
  2. Has Jazz been the subject of any regulatory investigation or enforcement action?
    Enforcement history bears on a supplier's conduct risk.
  3. Is Jazz party to any material litigation?
    Material litigation can affect a supplier's finances and continuity.
  4. Has Jazz had any security incident affecting its own systems or customer data, and how was it disclosed?
    An endpoint agent that records user activity makes the supplier's own security record important.
  5. Has Jazz lost any customer since launch, and why?
    Loss of major customers can indicate product or service problems.
  6. Have any founders or senior executives left Jazz, or been subject to adverse findings?
    Leadership instability affects delivery and support.
  7. What is Jazz's pricing basis and list price, including the endpoint agent and the CrowdStrike Foundry app?
    Without a pricing basis a bank cannot estimate cost at enterprise scale.
  8. Does Jazz discover and classify sensitive data at rest in file stores, SaaS and cloud, or only data handled on endpoints?
    A bank needs to know whether the product finds sensitive data at rest or only watches it move.
Show the other 18
  1. Does Jazz offer an API, webhook or streaming export of findings and audit records?
    Events must reach the bank's own monitoring to be useful in investigations.
  2. Which SIEM products does Jazz integrate with, beyond returning findings to CrowdStrike Falcon?
    SIEM integration is needed to correlate data-loss events with other security telemetry.
  3. Which assurance reports (for example SOC 2 or ISO/IEC 27001) can Jazz provide, and what records can be exported for audit?
    Auditors need assurance reports and exportable records.
  4. In which regions can Jazz host a customer's data, and can the customer choose?
    A bank may need captured endpoint content to stay in a chosen jurisdiction.
  5. Can Jazz provide its sub-processor list with purposes and locations, including any AI model providers?
    Sub-processors are fourth parties that may handle the bank's data.
  6. Is what Melody learns from one customer's data kept to that customer alone, and is any customer data used to train shared models?
    The investigator learns how each organisation works, so the bank must know where that learning is kept.
  7. How does Jazz encrypt captured endpoint data at rest and in transit, on the device and in its cloud?
    Encryption at rest and in transit is a baseline control.
  8. Can a customer supply and control the encryption keys for data Jazz stores?
    Customer-held keys limit the supplier's access to stored data.
  9. Has Jazz had any breach of customer data?
    Past breaches indicate how well the supplier protects what it holds.
  10. Does Jazz publish service status and incident history?
    Outage history shows how reliable the service is.
  11. Which AI models power Melody, who provides them, and where do they run?
    Third-party model providers are fourth parties that may process the bank's data.
  12. What support tiers, hours and response targets does Jazz offer?
    Support hours and response targets determine how quickly a fault is resolved.
  13. On termination, what data can a customer export from Jazz, and how are endpoint agents and stored data removed?
    A bank needs to know how findings are exported and agents removed.
  14. From which countries do Jazz staff access customer data?
    Support staff location determines who can access the bank's data.
  15. Which third-party attestations does Jazz hold, with what scope, auditor and dates?
    Independent attestations are the main public evidence of a young supplier's controls.
  16. Can Jazz export findings and audit records by API or stream, which SIEM products does it support, and how long are records kept?
    Without knowing whether findings can be exported or sent to a SIEM, the product cannot be placed on the observability scale.
  17. Which SOC report and which ISO certificate does Jazz hold, who issued them, what is in scope and when do they expire?
    The badges on the vendor's home page do not identify a report or certificate, so no attestation can be counted.
