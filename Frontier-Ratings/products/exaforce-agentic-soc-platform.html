How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. Has Exaforce been subject to any regulatory investigation, enforcement action or order in the last five years?
    Regulatory action against a supplier is a due-diligence finding in its own right.
  2. Is Exaforce party to any material litigation, pending or resolved in the last five years?
    Material litigation can affect a supplier's finances and its ability to keep serving the bank.
  3. Has Exaforce had any security incident affecting customer data or its production service, and what was disclosed to customers?
    A security product supplier's own incident record bears directly on the bank's exposure.
  4. Has Exaforce lost any large or regulated customer in the last two years, and why?
    Departures of large customers can signal product or service problems.
  5. Have there been any departures, disputes or conduct findings involving Exaforce's senior leadership in the last two years?
    Leadership disputes or misconduct can disrupt a supplier.
  6. Has Exaforce reduced its workforce in the last two years, and by how much?
    Workforce reductions can affect support and product development.
  7. How is the Exaforce platform priced (by data volume, users, assets or investigations), and what does the managed service add?
    Log volume and seat pricing drive the monitoring bill.
  8. In what format does Exaforce store customer telemetry, and can the customer export all of it in an open format or hold it in its own storage?
    Open storage formats and bulk export limit lock-in.
Show the other 7
  1. By what API or stream can a customer export Exaforce's detections, investigation records and audit logs?
    The bank needs the platform's own records in its archive.
  2. In which regions can an Exaforce tenant be hosted, and can the customer choose?
    The bank must know where its security telemetry is stored.
  3. Does Exaforce support customer-managed encryption keys?
    Some banks require control of encryption keys.
  4. Has Exaforce had any breach of customer data?
    Past breaches indicate how the vendor protects customer data.
  5. In which countries is customer data processed by Exaforce and each of its sub-processors, including AI model inference?
    Processing location determines which laws apply to the bank's telemetry.
  6. From which countries do Exaforce support staff and managed-service analysts access customer data?
    Analysts and support staff may view customer telemetry from other jurisdictions.
  7. Can Exaforce provide documentation of its audit trail, log export options and retention settings?
    The bank needs to know what record the platform keeps of its own automated actions.
