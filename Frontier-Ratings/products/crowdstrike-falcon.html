Cloud-delivered endpoint security on the Falcon platform, using a single sensor on each endpoint managed from a web console. Modules include Falcon Prevent (next-generation antivirus), Falcon Insight XDR (detection and response) and Falcon Complete (managed detection and response), with Falcon Next-Gen SIEM for wider data. The vendor's site now markets the line as agentic endpoint security.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

  2. Has CrowdStrike lost any large enterprise or financial-services customers in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of large customers can signal product or service problems.
  3. Have there been any unplanned executive departures, misconduct findings or governance disputes at CrowdStrike in the last 24 months?
    Leadership instability or misconduct can affect vendor direction and reputation.
  4. Does CrowdStrike Falcon keep a tamper-evident audit trail of administrator and response actions, and can it be exported?
    Audit trails support investigations and regulatory evidence.
  5. How does CrowdStrike Falcon explain to the customer why a detection or automated response action was taken?
    Analysts and auditors need to understand why a detection or automated response fired.
  6. Can CrowdStrike provide the current list of sub-processors for CrowdStrike Falcon, with purpose and processing location for each?
    A bank must know which fourth parties process its data.
  7. Is customer data from CrowdStrike Falcon used to train or tune machine-learning or generative AI models, and if so are those models shared across customers and can a customer opt out?
    A bank must know whether its telemetry trains models shared with other customers.
  8. How is customer data in CrowdStrike Falcon encrypted in transit and at rest, and who manages the keys?
    Encryption in transit and at rest is a baseline control for bank data.
