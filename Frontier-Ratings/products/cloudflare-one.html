Cloudflare's SASE platform, delivered from the same global network as its other services. It combines zero-trust network access, a secure web gateway with TLS decryption, CASB, data loss prevention, remote browser isolation, email security and WAN-as-a-service (Cloudflare WAN, formerly Magic WAN), with a device client formerly called WARP.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (10)

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. Which large Cloudflare One customers have left or materially reduced their use in the last two years, including after the November and December 2025 outages?
    Loss of large customers can signal product or service problems before they show elsewhere.
  2. For any allowed or blocked request, do the logs show which policy and which verdict produced the decision?
    A bank must be able to explain why a connection was allowed or blocked.
  3. Is Cloudflare One customer traffic, log or content data used to train or tune AI or machine-learning models, and if so are those models shared across customers and can a customer opt out?
    Inspected traffic and logs contain sensitive bank data; use for model training must be understood.
  4. How are Cloudflare One logs and any retained content encrypted at rest, and with what key management?
    Stored logs and any retained content need protection at rest and in transit.
  5. Can a customer hold or manage the encryption keys for stored Cloudflare One data, beyond choosing where TLS private keys are kept with Geo Key Manager?
    Key control determines who can read stored data.
  6. Which third-party cloud providers does Cloudflare One depend on, and what does Google LLC process for Zero Trust according to the sub-processor list?
    Hosting dependencies are fourth parties a bank must record for concentration risk.
  7. Which third-party AI model providers, if any, do Cloudflare One features depend on?
    Third-party AI model dependencies are fourth parties a bank must record.
  8. What exit assistance does Cloudflare provide for Cloudflare One, in what format can policy configuration and logs be exported, and how long is data retained after termination?
    Replacing an inline security service is a major change; exit terms and export of policy matter.
Show the other 2
  1. From which countries do Cloudflare staff and contractors provide support for Cloudflare One, and can support access be limited to chosen jurisdictions?
    Where support staff sit determines who can see customer data during troubleshooting.
  2. Please provide documentation covering encryption of stored Cloudflare One data, customer-managed keys and any use of customer data for model training?
    Data risk cannot be placed without knowing encryption at rest and model-training use.
