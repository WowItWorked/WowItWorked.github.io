Third-party risk management platform, formerly the Prevalent TPRM Platform and now part of Mitratech's risk and compliance range. It centres on supplier assessments: a library of questionnaire templates, AI-assisted completion and review of assessments, workflow and remediation tracking, and mapping of results to regulations and control frameworks. It adds continuous monitoring of suppliers' cyber, financial, operational and reputational signals, libraries of completed assessments (Vendor Intelligence Networks), contract management and optional managed services. Multi-tenant SaaS on AWS.

Named customers

Organisations named in public sources as using this product, strongest evidence first. This is who has been confirmed, not a complete customer list.

No named customer could be confirmed from public sources. Vendors often cannot name bank customers, so this is not a finding about adoption.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Open as a question list
  1. In what year was Prevalent, Inc. founded, and how long has the current platform been generally available?
    Company age is an input to maturity.
  2. How many Mitratech employees work on the Prevalent product in engineering, managed services and support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a large bank deployment.
  3. Has headcount on the Prevalent product been reduced since the October 2024 acquisition, and by how much?
    Post-acquisition staff cuts can reduce support and product capacity.
  4. Is Prevalent or Mitratech party to any material litigation, current or concluded in the last five years?
    Material litigation can affect a supplier's finances and continuity.
  5. Has the Prevalent platform or Mitratech had any security incident affecting customer or supplier data in the last five years, and how was each disclosed?
    A third-party risk platform holds suppliers' security evidence, so its own incidents matter to many firms.
  6. Has the Prevalent product lost any large regulated customer since the October 2024 acquisition, and why?
    Loss of large customers after an acquisition can signal product or service problems.
  7. Which Prevalent executives and product leaders remain with Mitratech, and who now leads the product?
    Departures after an acquisition can affect product direction.
  8. How does the platform show the basis for AI-generated assessment answers, AI risk scores and false-positive filtering in monitoring?
    AI-suggested answers and risk scores need to be explainable to assessors and auditors.
