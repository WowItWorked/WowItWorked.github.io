A security detection product on Cribl.Cloud, announced in September 2026, that Cribl sells as a SIEM. It is covered here for its detection posture management: it maps the detections it runs to MITRE ATT&CK and shows which techniques are covered, which could be covered with data already collected, and which need new telemetry. This is the part that continues the CardinalOps platform, which Cribl acquired in July 2026 and which no longer has its own website. Cribl's documentation labels the product Preview and not recommended for production. The standalone CardinalOps platform worked across other vendors' SIEM and EDR tools; that is not documented for Cribl Detect.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Timeline

  1. 2026-09-29 · launch Cribl
    Cribl launches Cribl Detect, a SIEM that includes detection posture management

    Cribl announced Cribl Detect on 29 September 2026 and said it was available to Cribl.Cloud customers. The release lists detection posture management, which maps existing detections to MITRE ATT&CK and shows coverage gaps, broken or noisy rules and missing telemetry, alongside in-stream detections, federated investigation and AI-assisted triage.
