Third-party risk offering within Bitsight's cyber risk intelligence platform. It combines daily outside-in security ratings and continuous monitoring of suppliers with Vendor Risk Management workflows (questionnaires, evidence review, AI document analysis), automatic fourth-party discovery and a Trust Management Hub for sharing a company's own assessments. Sold as Continuous Monitoring, with or without Vendor Risk Management, in bands by vendor count.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Questions to ask the vendor (10)

Each is something that could not be established from public sources. Export them as a list to send, with empty columns for the vendor’s answer and the evidence supplied.

Export and share
Open as a question list
  1. How many full-time employees does Bitsight have, and how many work in engineering and customer support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a large bank deployment.
  2. Has Bitsight reduced headcount in the last 24 months, and by how much?
    Layoffs can reduce support and product capacity and signal financial pressure.
  3. Has Bitsight had any security incident affecting customer data or service in the last five years, and how was each disclosed and remediated?
    A supplier's own security incidents show how well it protects customer data.
  4. Has Bitsight lost any major financial-services customers in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of major customers can indicate product or service problems.
  5. Does Bitsight Third-Party Risk Management produce the DORA register of information or other supervisory third-party registers, and in which formats?
    EU and UK supervisors require registers of third-party arrangements; a bank needs to know whether the product produces them.
  6. What user and system activity logs does Bitsight make available to customers, for how long are they retained, and how can they be exported?
    A bank needs activity logs to investigate misuse and to meet its own audit requirements.
  7. Does Bitsight support customer-managed encryption keys for data held in Vendor Risk Management?
    Customer-managed keys let a bank revoke a supplier's access to its data.
  8. Has Bitsight had any breach of customer data in the last five years, and what was disclosed to customers?
    A history of breaches of customer data is a direct indicator of data risk.
Show the other 2
  1. Does Bitsight publish a service status page or incident history, and what outages occurred in the last 24 months?
    Outage history shows how reliable the service is and how openly the vendor reports problems.
  2. From which countries do Bitsight support and operations staff access customer data?
    Where support staff sit determines who can access the bank's data and under which law.
