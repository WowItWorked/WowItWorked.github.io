Workflow platform for the third-party risk lifecycle: intake and onboarding, configurable assessments and questionnaires, issue remediation, monitoring and reporting, with AI agents, Assessment Autofill and an Evidence Evaluator for supplier documents. It draws on the vendor's Global Risk Exchange, a controls-based dataset of supplier risk profiles that came with the 2023 CyberGRX merger, and its Risk Index score, and connects to outside ratings and data providers.

How confidence is setIt follows the kind of evidence, not judgement.
  • The customer's own statement (high confidence at most): The organisation says so itself: its press release, website, annual report or a named executive on the record.
  • Regulatory or securities filing (high confidence at most): A filing by the customer or the vendor names the relationship.
  • Independent press (high confidence at most): Editorially independent reporting describes the deployment.
  • Joint announcement (medium confidence at most): A press release issued with the vendor, usually with a quote from the customer.
  • Vendor case study (medium confidence at most): The vendor's published customer story naming the organisation.
  • Vendor mention only (low confidence at most): A logo, a customer list or a passing mention by the vendor, with nothing from the customer.
  • An organisation that also holds a stake in the vendor is labelled “Investor and customer” and is never rated above medium.
  • A partnership is listed as a partner, not a customer.

Open as a question list
  1. How many full-time employees does ProcessUnity have, and how many work in engineering and customer support?
    Headcount indicates capacity to support a large bank deployment.
  2. Has ProcessUnity reduced headcount in the last 24 months, and by how much?
    Layoffs can reduce support and product capacity and signal financial pressure.
  3. Is ProcessUnity party to any material litigation, including intellectual-property or customer disputes?
    Material litigation can affect a supplier's finances and its ability to deliver.
  4. Has ProcessUnity had any security incident affecting customer data or service in the last five years, and how was each disclosed and remediated?
    A supplier's own security incidents show how well it protects customer data.
  5. Has ProcessUnity lost any major financial-services customers in the last 24 months, and why?
    Loss of major customers can indicate product or service problems.
  6. Have any current or former ProcessUnity executives or directors been the subject of misconduct findings or disputes with the company?
    Leadership disputes or misconduct can disrupt a supplier and harm its customers' reputation.
  7. When did ProcessUnity last receive new capital, from whom, and how much?
    The date and size of the latest capital event indicate how well funded the vendor is.
  8. Does the ProcessUnity TPRM Platform integrate with SIEM products, and what events can be exported?
    A bank's security operations team may want supplier risk events in its own SIEM.
Show the other 7
  1. Is customer data, including assessments and supplier evidence, used to train or fine-tune any AI model, and which model providers process it?
    A bank must know whether its supplier assessments and documents are used to train AI models.
  2. Does ProcessUnity support customer-managed encryption keys?
    Customer-managed keys let a bank revoke a supplier's access to its data.
  3. Has ProcessUnity or the former CyberGRX had any breach of customer data in the last five years, and what was disclosed to customers?
    A history of breaches of customer data is a direct indicator of data risk.
  4. Does ProcessUnity publish a service status page or incident history, and what outages occurred in the last 24 months?
    Outage history shows how reliable the service is and how openly the vendor reports problems.
  5. Which AI model providers do the ProcessUnity AI agents, Evidence Evaluator and Assessment Autofill use?
    AI model providers are fourth parties that process the bank's data.
  6. What data export formats, post-termination data access, deletion commitments and transition assistance does ProcessUnity offer?
    A bank must be able to retrieve its third-party records and leave without disruption.
  7. Can ProcessUnity provide documentation of its audit trail, log export and SIEM integration options?
